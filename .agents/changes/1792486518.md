# Settings and the first launch

> Change `1792486518` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Roadmap step 2: the Settings window, General and Dock first, and the first-launch sequence of
[onboarding](../product/onboarding.md), whose choices take effect only at its end.

## Implementation

**SwiftUI for the ordinary windows only.** Settings and the first launch are standard windows, which is
what SwiftUI is for; the stage and everything on it stay AppKit, where the glass, the overlay's blending
and the keyboard need control SwiftUI does not give. dock2 switches to a regular app while either window is
open and back to an accessory when both close.

**Settings** shows only the sections whose features exist; Search, Clipboard, Files, Shortcuts, Plugins and
Permissions arrive with them. `SettingsModel` is an `@Observable` bridge over `SettingsStore`: a binding per
key, and a generation counter bumped by `UserDefaults.didChangeNotification` so a change from the platter's
menu redraws the window. The summon picker offers ⌘space "in place of Spotlight", a recorded chord, or none;
its default is none, since the product offers ⌘space and never assumes it.

**The first launch** moved ahead of everything that changes the Mac. `AppController` now reads the journal
(which changes nothing), gives back what a dead run held if `onboarded` is unset, shows the sequence, and
only on its last page copies the system Dock, writes the choices and starts the guard, the takeovers and the
stage. The preview on page 2 is a `DockPreview` — the same layout, glass and icons — over a gradient: over
white the glass reads flat, and `desktopImageURL` returned `/System/Library/CoreServices/DefaultDesktop.heic`
here, not the wallpaper showing.

**A launch crash found by building the bundle.** `UserDefaults(suiteName: "zip.tanner.dock2")` is nil inside
a bundle whose identifier is that same string, so `dist/dock2.app` trapped at start while `.build` runs
were fine. The store now uses the standard defaults inside the bundle.

Verified on this Mac: the sequence page by page with keys interlocked on dock2 being frontmost (the files
page's "Not now" and the login page's "Not Now" by click, to open no System Settings pane and register no
login item); its choices written and dock2 started only at the end; quitting in the middle leaving no
journal and `com.apple.dock` as it was; Settings opened from the platter's menu, a toggle written through
and read back, ⌘W closing it and dock2 back to an accessory app; `dist/dock2.app` launching through
LaunchServices once fixed.

## Observations

- A dock2 started from a terminal is not allowed to come forward (cooperative activation), so its first
  launch window opens inactive; launched from Finder it is in front. Clicks on an inactive window reach its
  buttons only sometimes, which makes the sequence awkward to drive in tests.
- "Just Desktop, Documents and Downloads" raises macOS's folder prompts and "Open System Settings" opens
  the Full Disk Access pane; neither was exercised, to leave this Mac's privacy settings alone. Neither is
  acted on beyond recording the choice until Files.
- Open at login cannot register a `.build` binary; it was not exercised from the bundle.
- Collect logs was not exercised: it reveals the file in Finder.
- The delays read "0.19 s" and "0.04 s": dock2's own values, which allow for its latency, not the 220 and
  57 ms seen on screen.
