# Glass toasts that ask

> Change `1792567577` — 2026-10-21

## Goal

Copy Contents' 10 MB question (`1792567010`) was first a row in Files' banner. That row pushed the columns
down as it came and went, and the user disliked it. They also found the panel's toast, an opaque dark pill
with white text, out of keeping with the rest of dock2. They liked what both were for, and pointed at Fiber's
toasts (`~/Developer/fiber`) for how a toast should look and take input.

## Implementation

**What Fiber does.** `CloseWindowNotice` is a panel of rimmed glass (`.glassEffect` twice, a rim's width
apart) over the same `PanelShadow` its other panels have. It holds one line of 13 pt secondary text with the
key picked out, drops 6 pt into place as it fades in (a 0.35 s spring, no bounce, the fade 0.2 s), lifts as it
fades out (ease-in, 0.3 s), and lets clicks through. Its interactive notices take the pointer. Its prompts'
buttons are `.glass` / `.glassProminent`, each with its key ("↩", "esc") after the title at 11 pt medium and
55 % opacity.

**What dock2 does.** `Feedback` keeps its three uses (toast, confirmation, notice) and their timing. It draws
them all in one `Toast`:

- **The capsule** is 36 pt high, 10 pt from the panel's edge, so its 18 pt radius is concentric with the
  panel's 28.5 pt.
- **The glass** is dock2's own `.label` recipe, measured from the Dock's name bubbles, in place of SwiftUI's
  `.glassEffect`. dock2's glass is the measured `GlassLayer`, and the label recipe is the one already made for
  a small capsule with text.
- **The shadow** is `PanelShadow`, as Fiber's notices use their panels'.
- **Motion** is Fiber's: the spring approximated by an ease-out curve (0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1) over 0.35 s, and no
  travel under Reduce Motion.

**Motion by the layer, not the frame.** The drop was first the frame's origin animated through `animator()`.
The user saw the first toast after a launch show off to one side and slide into place. A frame given to a
layer-backed view reaches its layer only at the view's next update, so the animation set off from where the
layer last was. The first time, that was where the view began: the panel view's corner, with no size. Later
toasts set off from the last toast's place, close enough not to show. The frame is now set once, and the
drop and the lift are additive `position.y` animations on the layer (the lift held at its end until the toast
hides), with the fade an explicit `opacity` animation from the presentation's value. The toast is laid out
whole (`layoutSubtreeIfNeeded`) before it shows, since a button places its title in a layout pass of its own.
Recorded at 120 fps after a fresh launch, the first question now appears in place, drops and fades in over
about 0.2 s, its text and buttons there from the first frame.

**Buttons.** A confirmation carries Cancel and its action as `ToastButton`s, a `PillButton` subclass, so they
light under the pointer through the panel's `Hoverable` path like Files' bar. They are 26 pt high, inset
5 pt (concentric again):

- **Cancel** has a faint ink fill.
- **The action** is in the accent colour, or `systemRed` when `Confirmation.destructive`.
- **Each** has its key dimmed after its title, as Fiber's are.

`PillButton`'s `fill` became overridable and `isPressed` readable for this. A toast with buttons takes clicks
on its capsule; one without lets them through to the rows beneath, as Fiber's close-window notice does.

**Callers.** `confirm` took a title and showed "Title?  ↩ to confirm, esc to cancel" on red. It now takes a
`Feedback.Confirmation`, so a question can be other than the action's title. Search's and views' destructive
actions pass "Title?" with the title as the button. Files' delete-at-once asks "Delete “x” at once?" with
Delete. Copy Contents asks "“big.log” is 12 MB." with Copy Contents, not destructive. The banner row from
`1792567010` is gone, and Files' banner is as it was before that change.

**Verified** live, with keys posted into dock2:

- ⌘C in Files showed "Copied" as glass over the columns.
- ⌘K → Copy Contents on a 12 MB file asked with Cancel `esc` and an accent Copy Contents `↩`, and ↩ copied
  12,000,000 bytes.
- ⌥⌘⌫ asked "Delete “delete-me.txt” at once?" with a red Delete. Esc cancelled it, leaving the file and
  leaving Files open.
- The same in the light appearance (`DOCK2_APPEARANCE=light`): a bright capsule with dark text.
- With the pointer, the user's hands off: Cancel's fill lightened under it (about 9 levels, as the path bar's
  does) and the accent button showed its rollover. A click on Cancel closed the question, nothing copied and
  the row beneath untouched. A click on Copy Contents copied the 12 MB. A click on a "Copied" toast went
  through to the row under it.

## Observations

- The notice after a dismissal, glass over the desktop rather than over the panel, was not looked at.
- The toast covers the top row of what is beneath it while it shows. That is where it always sat; it is now
  see-through.
- A confirmation still gives way to any other key, but not to a click elsewhere in the panel.
