# The app icon

> Change `1792652764` — 2026-10-22

## Goal

The bundle had no icon, so Finder, Spotlight, the Applications folder and the privacy lists in System Settings
showed the generic one. dock2 has no Dock tile of its own (`LSUIElement`), so those places, mostly at 16 to
32 px, are where its icon is seen.

## Implementation

The icon is a 2 whose foot is the dock. `Resources/dock2.icon` is an Icon Composer document with two glass
groups, the tiles and their dots over one body that is the 2 and the platter, on a violet gradient plate, with
the system's dark plate in dark mode. `make app` compiles it with actool into `Assets.car` and `dock2.icns`,
named by `CFBundleIconName` and `CFBundleIconFile`, the way bario does. actool insists on writing a partial
plist; it names what `Info.plist` already does and goes to `dist/`, uncopied. `Resources/icon.py`, a uv script,
writes the document. The build never runs it, and its output is committed.

- **The 2** is Inter ExtraBold (`wght` 800, `opsz` 32), which is OFL. It is fetched from google/fonts at a fixed
  commit and checked by SHA-256 into `.build/icon`, not committed. SF Pro was ruled out because its licence
  covers UI mockups only. Rubik, Manrope, Nunito and Outfit were compared, and all read at 16 px.
- **The dock** is drawn to `IMPLEMENTATION.md` §1 at tile size 48: a 68 pt platter, 52 pt slots with 8 pt at
  each end, and running dots 6 pt above the bottom. The icons are 412 of 512 px of their tile, measured with
  `NSWorkspace.icon(forFile:)` on Finder, Notes and Xcode. The dots are drawn at 1.5× the Dock's 4 pt, because
  at true size the glass broke each into two specks at icon scale.
- **The fit.** The glyph's base bar is cut off at its top, found where the glyph narrows below 80% of the bar's
  width going up, and the platter takes its place. The platter runs from where the diagonal lands to 0.18 × its
  height past the bowl's right edge, and its height follows from that width at the four-tile ratio, 224:68. That
  height came out at 0.96 to 1.19 × each face's own bar, which is why the platter reads as the 2's foot. The
  diagonal lands 40 px below the platter's top, and the platter's top-left is cut along the diagonal's line, so
  the diagonal runs into the platter's left end with no shelf. The first tile clears that cut by 28 px; at
  56 px it cleared it by 10, which looked cramped. The corners are a sixth of the platter's height.
- **One body.** The 2 and the platter are traced as one outline. The concave joint inside the 2 is rounded to
  0.7 × the corner radius by a morphological closing, and the landing corner to 6 px by an opening, both on a
  4× raster traced back with `find_contours`, within a quarter pixel of the vectors. Tracing only the fillets
  and unioning them back left specks along the shoulder.

Rejected on the way: nine other directions, including the field's three forms, a folder, and the platter alone.
Also rejected: the 2 as separate glass over the platter, whose cut edge showed through as a seam; a platter
running well past the bowl, which read as a tail; a platter spanning the font's whole bar, which left a shelf
where the diagonal landed; and the Dock's own corner radius, a third of the height, which read softer than type.

Every candidate was judged on exports from Icon Composer's `ictool`, which lives at
`Xcode.app/Contents/Applications/Icon Composer.app/Contents/Executables/ictool` (`--export-image` with
`--rendition Default|Dark|ClearLight|ClearDark|TintedLight|TintedDark`), in all six renditions and at 16, 32
and 64 px.

## Observations

- A Command Line Tools-only build has no icon; the CI and release runners have Xcode.
- The dark, clear and tinted appearances are Icon Composer's own conversions of the light design, not tuned
  by hand. They were looked at in `ictool`'s exports, not on a desktop.
- Regenerating needs the network once, for the font. The geometry is fitted to Inter: another face would need
  the landing depth and the first tile's clearance checked again.
- The built bundle's icon was checked through `NSWorkspace.icon(forFile:)` on `dist/Dock2.app`. It was not
  checked in Finder over an older installed `Dock2.app`, where LaunchServices may hold the generic icon until
  the app is registered again.
