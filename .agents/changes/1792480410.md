# Files, in place of Finder's windows

> Change `1792480410` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Take in the Finder window that the Dock opens: a column browser in dock2's panel, two tabs at most, that
comes and goes with the dock. And find out how much of macOS's traffic to Finder can be sent there instead.
The design is `.agents/product/files/`.

## Implementation

Each claim was tried on this Mac (macOS 27.0.1) with a throwaway viewer app, registered with LaunchServices
from `~/Applications` so that its bundle id resolved, and every setting it needed was put back after each
step.

**"Show in Finder" can be sent to dock2.** With the global `NSFileViewer` default naming the viewer,
`NSWorkspace.activateFileViewerSelecting`, `selectFile(_:inFileViewerRootedAtPath:)` and `open -R`, each
called from another process, launched the viewer when it was not running and sent it `aevt/odoc` carrying
the file to reveal. No Finder window appeared. The event is the ordinary open-documents one, and dock2
claims no document types, so dock2 reads every one it receives as a reveal. A wildcard Apple Event handler
alone never saw it: AppKit's own `odoc` handler outranks a wildcard, so the handler has to be installed for
`aevt/odoc` itself.

**Folders cannot be.** Making the viewer the default app for `public.folder` failed with `paramErr` from both
`LSSetDefaultRoleHandlerForContentType` and `NSWorkspace.setDefaultApplication(at:toOpen:)`, although it
declared folders as a viewer. `open <folder>` and `NSWorkspace.open` went on opening Finder. A Finder
window's `AXDocument` is nil, so a stray window cannot be mapped back to its folder through Accessibility,
and dock2 does not try.

**The flip was tried and not taken.** `NSGlassEffectView` under a perspective rotation about its horizontal
axis — 0°, 40°, 75° — still renders as glass: the shape projects and the backdrop blurs. Its content loses
its vibrancy mid-turn, though, and a card flip wants two faces of one size, which the field (57 pt) and
Files are not. Files grows out of the platter like every other view.

**Access.** The folder finding from the search investigation applies to a browser twice over: it needs
Desktop, Documents and Downloads, one prompt each, and more besides. Hence Full Disk Access, asked for when
Files first opens, with the three folders as the fallback and locked places without either. That reverses
the earlier "never asked for" in `system/permissions.md`.

**Decided with the user**: Files is in the MVP; it grows rather than flips; it keeps its place while dock2
runs and opens at Home after a restart; ⌘⇧V moves, as ⌥⌘V does in Finder; "Show in Finder" is a takeover
the user opts into.

## Observations

- Unverified: whether Put Back works for what dock2 trashes; whether external and network volumes prompt
  without Full Disk Access; whether a Full Disk Access grant takes effect without reopening dock2; whether
  hot key entry 65 is ⌥⌘space; whether apps that reveal by scripting Finder directly honour `NSFileViewer`.
- Files' size, its row density and the copy engine's behaviour across volumes are for a prototype to settle.
- The viewer was unregistered and removed from `~/Applications`; `NSFileViewer` was left absent and the folder
  handler Finder, as they were found; the one Finder window the probes opened was closed.
