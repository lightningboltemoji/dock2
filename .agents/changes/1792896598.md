# Emptying the field, without a wobble or a flash

> Change `1792896598` — 2026-10-24

## Goal

Emptied, the field wobbled back to its form, and cleared with Esc it flashed. `1792869276` made the pour a
controlled expansion but left emptying as it was, noting that it might read as sloppy; it does.

- **The lava lamp's draw-in wobbled.** Measured in `FieldMotion` from a field with a row of results, deleted to
  nothing: the body collapsed from 640 to 149 pt in 0.13 s on a spring at 0.45 of critical damping. It passed its
  resting 40 pt (down to 36–38), sprang back into a 65 pt pill at about 0.8 s, and settled at 41 by 1 s, its
  height bouncing at 0.4 of critical damping meanwhile. Its two droplets, let go to drift home on the rest's slow
  springs, were left up to 134 pt outside the bar as it drew in.
- **Esc flashed the placeholder.** The rest's change played the text as a ghost of what was cleared, but the
  next change, the results going a moment later, played it on the field again. The field was empty by then, so
  the fade showed its placeholder: "Search" at full strength, shrinking into the middle, and no ghost at all.
- **The form did not wait for the panel.** "Emptied with results showing, the form waits for the panel to shrink
  back to the field" was not what happened: the field empties before its results go, and the growth, still at
  its old target, counted as there. So the form rested at once. The bar drew in under the row still showing, and
  the magnifier left for the middle, 91 pt in the first frame on screen.
- **Emptied as the field alone, the form never rested.** That happens on deleting before any results came.
  `isSettled` ignored a rest that was due, so nothing stepped. After 3 s the driver's loop snapped the poured
  field to the rest.

The first README take made the wobble look worse: shot as screenshots at irregular intervals and resampled to
30 fps, it dropped about one frame in four unevenly.

## Implementation

- **The body draws in as it pours out.** The lava lamp's width and height come in critically damped at the
  pour's response, 0.42, so the body neither passes its size at rest nor springs back.
- **The droplets ride the ends in, melted.** While the body draws in they keep riding its ends at the depth
  they were tucked to when poured (`FormSpace.tucked`), by the same step that rides them while poured. Once the
  ends have carried them to the body's middle, they are let go onto the rest's springs and drift out of it as at
  rest. The body has nearly arrived by then (about 70 pt across), so they bud off the bead rather than trail the
  bar. Being let go keeps each droplet's place and speed, so nothing jumps.
- **A rejected version** gave the droplets the body's critically damped spring toward their resting places,
  so each would part from its end as the body arrived. Their resting places lie off the body's axis, though,
  and they stood out of it from when the body was still 250 pt wide, by up to 40 pt for most of the draw-in.
- **The ghost is the driver's state.** `FormDriver` keeps `ghosted` from a clearing rest until the next pour,
  and every change plays the text as the ghost while it is set. `FieldView.play` keeps the field itself hidden
  then, so its placeholder never shows.
- **A rest waits for the field, and comes when it is the field.** `FieldMotion` rests a pending form only when
  the panel has been asked to be the field and is within 1 pt of it (`isField`). A rest that is due counts as
  movement, so `isSettled` no longer stops a field emptied as the field alone from resting. `FormDriver` takes
  up the rest's loop only once the form has rested (`hasRested`), not while it waits on the panel.
- The rest the loop plays is untouched: let go onto its springs within about 0.6 s of resting, the motion
  settles into it long before `FormDriver` hands over at 4 s, as `emptiedItComesToRestAsTheLoopDoes` checks.
- **Tests.** `theLavaLampDrawsInWithoutWobbling` checks, at eighteen moments of the rest, that the body never
  gets narrower than its size at rest or wider again than drifting stretches it, and that no droplet stands
  out of the body while it is more than 64 pt across. Against the old emptying it fails thousands of times
  over. `emptiedItShrinksBackFirst` now also empties the field 1 ms and 50 ms before the panel is told to shrink.
  `emptiedAsTheFieldItRestsAtOnce` empties a poured field that never grew. Both fail on the old condition.

## Observations

- After: no droplet out of the body until it is about 62 pt across, at about 0.6 s; the bead at 41 pt by 0.85 s.
  Sampled at 60 Hz as the driver plays it, from 33 moments of the rest, emptied by Esc and by deleting, no shape
  moves out of line with its neighbours and no stretched droplet visibly turns between samples.
- Filmed at up to 120 fps: Esc from "25 mi/hr to km/hr" now shows the cleared words drift into the middle as they
  fade, the magnifier holding its place while the panel shrinks back, then the bar drawing in. One frame of that
  recording was torn along straight lines. ScreenCaptureKit's stream redraws only what changed (`OBSERVING.md`),
  and the model has no such jump, so that frame is the capture's.
- The drop and the gem draw in as they did, underdamped. The drop's fronts stand up to 41 pt out of its bar for
  about 0.6 s. The same treatment would suit them, but they were not filmed and were left.
- The README take is shot again from screenshots on a fixed clock, so each frame is a moment one frame on.
