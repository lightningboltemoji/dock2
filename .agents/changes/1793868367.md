# Files' place is one control

> Change `1793868367` — 2026-11-05

## Goal

Only the plate raised the places' menu, a 22 pt target beside a name that looks as if it should. Asked to have the icon and
the Home label act as one, with no capsule taller than the others. This takes back part of `1793864209`, which split the two
so that `Home` went Home, and rejected a capsule around the selector. What that change objected to was a crumb that could not
go Home and a pop-up signed only by ⌃⌄. The pill here has no accessory, and it is not a crumb any more: it is the place.

## Implementation

**The pill is the place, not the first crumb.** The plate and the first crumb were never the same thing: the plate is the
place holding the folder, the crumb the path's root, so `~/Documents/x` showed Documents' plate beside `Home`. Fusing them as
they stood would have labelled an orange Documents plate "Home", and giving the plate the root's icon would lose the colour
that says where you are. So the pill names the place, and the breadcrumb is what lies inside it: `Home › Downloads › Chango`
is `[Downloads] › Chango`, `Macintosh HD › Applications › Utilities` is `[Applications] › Utilities`, and iCloud Drive,
`Home › Library › Mobile Documents › com~apple~CloudDocs` until now, is `[iCloud Drive]`.

`Place.start(along:in:)` finds where the place begins as the last run of the path held by one place. Comparing a segment's
URL with the place's was the obvious way and fails twice: the two differ by a trailing slash, and a server holds shares that
are not under its URL. It sits in `Dock2System` so it can be tested. `FilesScreen.segments` is unchanged, since the columns
still need the whole path, and `PathBar` takes off what the place stands for. The crumbs now fold from the first, as the
place is what always stays.

**The pill is a small row.** It is 28 pt, as crumbs and tabs are. The first build left the 21.75 pt plate and the title on the
glyph and text lines and drew a pill round them, which fitted and looked wrong: 3 pt above and below the plate and 18.5 pt to
the title. Those lines are 28 pt apart for a 56 pt row, so a 28 pt pill cannot keep both and hold its contents together.
`visual.md` already had the answer, a row's icon in a square as tall as the row with the text starting where the square ends,
which at 28 pt gives an 18 pt plate on the glyph line and a title 14 pt before the text line. The pill then moved 2 pt out of
that square, onto the margin, by eye: a capsule's end curves away from a plate's corner, so that side needs more than the top
and bottom do. Measured off captures in light, it is 28.0 pt tall, the plate 6.5 pt from the pill's end and 6.5 pt from the
title, and 5 pt above and below. \`PillButton.leading\` is that left inset, a pill's \`padding\` being the same at both ends.
Overriding \`layout()\` in \`PlaceButton\` was the alternative, and repeats \`intrinsicContentSize\` as well.

Each alternative was captured from the running app, light and dark, through a scratch build reading its metrics from a file.
Rejected: the same pill with corners concentric with the plate, as a row's band has, which stops matching the capsules beside
it; the same pill moved 14 pt right to keep the title on the text line, which takes the plate off the glyph line and indents
the whole bar at rest; a fill behind the title alone, the plate lifting, where nothing moves but it reads as a plate beside a
crumb; and no fill, the plate lifting and the title brightening, which answers differently from every crumb, shows nothing for
a title already in full colour, and cannot stay lit under a menu.

**One body.** The plate no longer lifts on hover: the fill answers now, and a plate swelling alone is the two parts acting
separately. The landing spring stays, moved to the icon's layer so the name does not bounce with it. The menu's edge meets the
pill's rather than the plate's, and `rise` keeps the pill lit until the menu closes, which `…` gets as well.

**Drops.** The old first crumb took drops, so the pill does, into the place. `NSImageView` registers for drags of its own even
when not editable, so the title half of the pill took a drop and the plate half lost it. A plain crumb was the control: same
file, same drag, and it landed. `PillButton` unregisters its icon, which also fixes a tab's plate, the same dead spot.

Driven on the debug build, light and dark. A lit pill and a lit crumb cover the same pixel rows, 28.0 pt, as tab pills do. The
title half and the plate half both raise the menu and both take a drop, as does the pill's left end and a tab's plate. Choosing from the menu goes
there, Esc leaves things as they were, a crumb goes to its folder, `…` lists what it folded, right up to the place. Home,
Documents, Downloads, Applications, `/`, `/usr/local`, iCloud Drive and a Synology share each read as above, the share keeping
its crumb (`Synology › Media`). An item selected takes the path's full colour from the pill. A typed path's glyph stands where the
plate does. On a change of place the plate's edge dips 2 pt, overshoots by half a point and settles.

## Observations

- A click on the pill no longer goes to the place. Back to Home from inside it is the menu's first row or ⇧⌘H, two actions
  where a crumb took one. That is what was asked for, and the cost of it.
- Dropping into an ancestor of the place has no target now: from `~/Documents`, nothing in the bar is Home.
- The path no longer says where a place sits. Downloads does not say it is in Home, though the columns do.
- The title stands 14 pt before the text line, so the text steps right when a path is typed or a search starts, and changes
  size as it did. The plate is also smaller than the magnifier that stands in its spot.
- Not driven: VoiceOver's reading of the pill, and a place's name long enough to truncate.
- Still no test target for `Dock2Shell`, so `layoutCrumbs` rests on these runs. `Place.start` is tested.
