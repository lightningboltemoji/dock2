# The band fits its icon: corners of 34, 28 and 20

> Change `1793861542` — 2026-11-04

## Goal

The selected row's corners felt awkward against the pane's, in a way that was hard to name. They were concentric, 22 and
14 pt, and drawn exactly so: a fit of the band gives 14.0, and the gap to the pane holds 8 pt round the turn to within 0.1.
So the question was whether the radii were un-Apple, or whether something else was off.

## Implementation

**What Apple does.** The system's own surfaces, fitted from pixels on macOS 27.2. Spotlight: panel 36.5, band 12 pt in and
24.5, so exactly the panel less the gap. A menu: 12.25, highlight 5 pt in and 7, the same again. Our rule was the system's.
The difference is one level further in. Spotlight's plate is 35.5 pt with corners of 9.5, 13.5 pt inside the band, and 9.5
+ 13.5 is 23: its band is nearly concentric with its icon too. Ours was 8.3 + 12 = 20.3 against a band of 14, so the 12 pt
around the icon swelled to 14.2 at the corner, where Spotlight's stays within 4%. A band that fits what is outside it and
not what is inside it was what felt off. The proportion followed: a band of 0.25 of its height against Spotlight's 0.40,
reading as a rounded rectangle above a field with round ends.

**The corners.** `rowHeight / 2` was the ground's radius and is now the pane's. The ground is that plus the rim, 34, the
band that less the padding, 20, and 20 less the 12 pt inside the band lands on the plate's own 8. Both gaps then hold within
1%, and 28, 20 and 8 are on the grid. Rejected, from the real app side by side: 18 or 20 on the band alone, which moves the
swell outward, to 21% or 31% against the pane; and 36, 30, 22, Spotlight's proportion, which looks about the same and leaves
the grid. A capsule band was never a candidate: past the pane's own radius, it turns the corners inside out.

**The growth.** The field stays a capsule of 28, so the body's radius has to change as it grows. It needs no animation of
its own. `layering` already draws the clear glass and the pane in by `paneInset × ground`, so adding that same amount to
the body's radius (`FieldMotion.frame`) leaves them at 28 throughout: the rim opens around the glass, and only the ground's
corners move. It stops at half the extent, or a body that is low while its ground is solid, as a screen opened from nothing
is, would ask for corners past its height. `PanelController.shape`'s radius is not read, since the driver takes only the
frame; it says the true value anyway.

**Compact rows.** They share the bands but not the answer: 20 on a 36 pt row is past a capsule. `Row.bandRadius` is
`height / 2 - plate / 4`, concentric with the plate, which is 20 for a regular row, so the pane's rule holds there as a
result and not by a second constant, and 13 for a compact one, the same 0.36 of the height. At a pane's corner a compact band
pinches its gap to the pane by 2.9 pt. Measured at 13, 14, 16 and 18, what is gained toward the pane is lost toward the icon,
and the icon is beside the band everywhere. Seen in its corner, it sits fine.

**The Actions menu** keeps its 19 pt corners, so its nested rows and its capsule are unchanged, and `radius - cornerRadius`
moves it to 15 pt in from the field's edges, from 9. Moving it back to 9 needs corners of 25, and then its 30 pt rows' bands
would want more than a capsule. Its foot now sits 1 pt above the pill's, too little to see in the morph.

**Left alone.** A grid's selection, 12 pt, was never concentric: the grid stands 16 pt in at its sides and 8 pt at its top. In
the pane's corner it looks as it did. Files' column bands stay Finder's 6 pt.

`dock2 debug capture`, before and after: search, a list, a list beside a preview, a grid, a detail, a form, Files and an open
Actions menu, and the same in dark. Drawn: ground 33.5, pane about 28 on its diagonal, band 19.5, compact band 13.0, where a
variant set to 20 also fits 19.5. Growth, shrinking and Files opening and closing were recorded and read close at the corner, with no
step. Removing the height limit fails `theGlassKeepsTheFieldsRadiusAsTheGroundComes`, and setting the radius outright fails
`theCornersNeverJump`.

## Observations

- Spotlight opened from `spotlight://apps` measured 520 wide, an 87 pt field and 62 pt rows, where `1792478443` and
  `1792496911` measured 640 × 57 and 56 pt. An update or the route could explain it; the sizes were not chased. The ratios
  are what this rests on.
- The Siri app's sidebar is not concentric: selection 8 pt, 10 pt in, in a window of 15.5. That selection is not in the window's
  corner. Concentric is what the system does where a selection meets one, not everywhere.
- Only the lava lamp was watched growing; the other forms have the unit tests. Reduce Motion was watched coming in, not going.
- `PanelShadow` paths the body with circular corners under continuous ones, as it did, on a larger corner now.
- The Files bar's pills and the field's Actions pill were concentric with the field's ends. They still are at the field's own
  28, not with the 34 of the panel's corner. It reads fine, and nothing was moved.
- `aWatchHearsChangesUntilItsFolderGoes` failed once, in a run with this change's code deliberately broken, and passed in the
  runs either side. It waits on a file watch and has nothing to do with corners: a timing flake to keep an eye on.
