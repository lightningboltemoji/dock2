# The panel's grid

> Change `1793778703` — 2026-11-03

## Goal

Nothing on the panel shared any geometry. The field was Spotlight's, measured off a screenshot: 640 × 57, a
23 pt magnifier from 22.5 pt, 26 pt text from 63 pt. Search's rows were Spotlight's too: 56 pt, a 36 pt icon
18 pt in, 16.5 pt titles from 72 pt, the band 10 pt in. Compact rows were 38 pt, a section's title was 20 pt
in, and Actions' pill was placed by the magnifier's ink. Dividers stopped short of
their ends at different insets on each axis. So the magnifier sat off the icons' centres, the field's text off
the titles, and the margins differed at the two edges. The icon looked small and loosely placed in its row, and
the field's type looked large. Files' folders crowded the glass's edge, and the panel had no principles a change
could be checked against.

The pane also read flatter than Actions' pill beside it. Its edge was lit top and bottom by Apple's diffuse
halo, which is not the same at both.

## Implementation

**The grid** (`PanelMetrics`, [visual](../product/visual.md#layout)) is a 4 pt unit for everything on the pane,
and three nested layers: the ground, the pane `paneInset` (6 pt) in from it, and a row's band `padding` (8 pt)
in from the pane. Their corners are 28, 22 and 14 pt, each the one outside it less the gap. A row (`PanelMetrics.Row`, which replaces
`RowsView.Metrics`) puts its icon in a square as tall as itself. Every horizontal measure follows from that:
the plate's x, the glyph's centre, the text's start, and a trailing end that mirrors the plate's leading one.
The field is the bottom row, 56 pt tall, so its magnifier and text stand on the rows' lines. From the panel's
edge, the **glyph line** is at 42 pt and the **text line** at 70 pt. The **margin** is 26 pt at both edges:
icons and sections' titles start on it, and trailing text, marks and the bar's pill stop on it. Type is
22/16/13/12. Compact rows went from a 16 pt plate to 20 pt, since 16 put the plate 18 pt in, off the grid.
`PanelGridTests` holds the grid's invariants rather than its numbers.

The pane stays 6 pt in, as glass-on-solid (`17a1ad4`) set it. The prototype had moved it to 8, onto the unit,
but at 8 the ground's rim looks off, which is why 6 had been chosen. So the unit governs what is on the pane,
measured from the pane's edge, and the rim outside it is tuned by eye. The lines measured from the panel's edge
follow the pane out, so the field's magnifier, its text and the pill stay on the rows' lines. The test checks the
pane's measures against the unit, and the corners for concentricity.

**Placing by ink.** `FieldLine` lays out the field's line and the path bar's. The magnifier is SF Symbols'
`magnifyingglass` at the field's 22 pt, regular, drawn at its own size (`.scaleNone`), so its 21.75 pt of ink is
centred on the glyph line. The text's capitals are centred on the midline: SF Pro's cap height at 22 pt is 15.5,
and the baseline is rounded to the half point. A text field's cell starts its text 2 pt in from its frame, a
label's and an editable one's alike, measured. That replaces an ink-based 3.5 pt, which put titles 1.5 pt off
the line. Rows take the same 2 pt off each text frame (`NSTextField.textInset`). The spinner is centred as far
from the trailing end as the magnifier from the leading one, or sits before the bar when one shows.

**The capsule** had a gap between its magnifier and its left end that was far too large, once the magnifier sat
42 pt in on the glyph line. Dropping the magnifier was considered, and matching Spotlight is a non-goal. Instead
the capsule rests at 70% of the field's width, centred and still, with its magnifier concentric with its left
end and its placeholder beside it. The first letter pours it out to the field's ends, critically damped at
0.42 s. The magnifier slides from the end's centre to the glyph line on the way, and the motion hides the
slide. `FormSim` gains `restsStill`, so a capsule at rest settles and doesn't loop, and `showsTextAtRest`, so its
placeholder is tracked at rest and rides out rather than landing as the other forms' first letter does. At rest
its hit area is its body. Emptied, nothing is ghosted: the placeholder stays.

**Rows.** An app's or a file's icon is drawn in a box of `plate / IconPlate.fraction`, 40 pt, so its plate is the
row's 32 pt. A view's list fits a symbol or a picture to the plate instead (`IconImages.row`), so it shows as
large as the app icons beside it rather than as large as their box. A section's title is 12 pt semibold over the
icons' leading edge. The × and the accessory end on the margin, 8 pt apart.

**The bar.** The pill's end is on the margin; the spinner sits 8 pt before it, and the field's text 8 pt before that. That moves the pill in,
as asked: it had sat by the magnifier's ink, nearer the end. The open menu is nested in the panel's corner,
concentric with it. Its edges are the panel's radius less its own (9 pt) in from the field's, wherever the pill
stands, where before they sat 5 pt out from the pill's.

**Dividers run edge to edge** across what they divide: a list beside its preview, Files' columns, the preview's
rule, and a detail's rule over its status. Stopping short left the vertical and horizontal lines inset by
different amounts.

**The pane's glass, in dark.** The face lifts from 0.045 to 0.07 of the way to white, still short of the bar's
capsule. It is lit from within by an inward `CASDFShadowEffect` (`GlassRecipe.Glow`) of white at 10% over 8 pt.
The glow needs `punchout`, or it lightens the ground outside the pane. It also needs a zero offset, since the
default (0, −10) brightens the top. Of a sheet of 8–48 pt at 4–16%, 8 pt at 10% was the one that read as soft
light rather than a line. A 1 pt glow and an outward shade glow drew a square band, darkening the sides.

The pane's lines are a 1 pt `CASDFKeyFillHighlightEffect`, key and fill both 0.16, spread 1.6, with `curvature` 2
so they taper smoothly round the corners. Its shade on the ground is the same at the top and the bottom (0.2).
Measured going out of the corner, the line rises 95–105 → 116 at the top and 95–105 → 114 at the bottom. The
recipe it replaced used the effect's diffuse halo, which lit the bottom edge's last pixel 168/255 against the
top's 135 with equal key and fill. Swapping the key and the fill changed nothing, so the halo favours an edge
facing down. On a toast it is 154 against 165. Hiding the field's clear glass entirely left
134 and 168 as they were, so the order the layers stack in is not the cause. The halo is not used anywhere now.
`curvature` below 1 saturates a straight edge to 254 at any amount. Light mode is unchanged: its face is white
already, and a glow adds nothing.

**Reduce Motion** shows every form as the capsule, so this is the field every Reduce Motion user sees. The
capsule changes at once (`isInstant`), and `PanelController` crossfades the panel over the slide. The first
take showed the placeholder at the field's text line and the typed letter at the capsule's, both on screen for
about six frames. The text follows the body through its own 0.06 s spring (`FormSpace.carry`), which ignored the
instant change. After an instant change, everything else was settled, so `advance` returned early and the text
moved only when the growth stepped it. The text now follows at once with an instant sim, and `pour` places it
there as it changes. `underReduceMotionTheCapsulesTextChangesWithIt` fails without the fix. The magnifier and
the text are placed by keyframes, which a `CATransition` does not dissolve. So under the fade they move at once,
while the glass and the text's content dissolve. A cut, with no crossfade, was also recorded. It is clean too,
but the design asks for fades in place of morphs.

## Observations

- Files' rows keep Finder's measured density (22 pt). Whether they join the grid is still **Open** in
  `visual.md`, and needs Finder measured again.
- The dark pane's sides have a dark outer pixel (34/255) that the top and bottom don't. It comes from a
  highlight the pane inherits from the panel's recipe: switching the face's `inputKeyFillHighlightAmount` off on
  the pane made that pixel 102. It is not tuned yet.
- Reduce Motion was recorded in dark only, and the typed letter brought results each time. A capsule that pours
  with no results to grow was not recorded.
