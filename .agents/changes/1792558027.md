# Files, made beautiful; tabs you can see

> Change `1792558027` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Files worked well and looked janky. Four problems: the glass grew around the columns while they already
floated, fully drawn, outside it; nothing had room from the panel's rounded corners, worst at the top
(the first row 6 pt from the edge); the places switcher was a folder glyph beside "~ ⌄", which said nothing;
and the overlay horizontal scroller sat over the rows. Separately, the user wanted tabs for copying
between places. The product had two, but the second appeared only after ⌘T, so they were invisible.

## Implementation

**The entrance.** Recorded at 120 fps: the screen's view was added at full size in the frame the glass
started growing, so for the slide's 240 ms the columns stood over the desktop. `PanelController` now puts a
screen's view and bar in a container (`content`) whose layer mask follows the glass. `setGlass` sets the
glass's shape and the mask's frame, corner radius and curve together, animated from the mask's
presentation, as `GlassLayer` animates its elements. The container fades in from a quarter of the way
through the growth. The field fades out under the bar rather than vanishing; first it was resized to
Files' width at once, so "Search" jumped outside the glass as it faded, and it now keeps its frame until
hidden. It is hidden once gone because a field at alpha 0 still takes clicks the bar passes through. Views
grow the same way, and got the same fix.

**Space.** `FilesLayout` insets everything 10 pt from the glass, so inner radii (Quick Look's, the banner's)
are 18.5, concentric with the panel's 28.5. The panel stays 960 × 520. Three columns narrow from Finder's
246 to 236 pt and the preview widens to 232, which keeps the first row's band clear of the corner curve.
The column hairlines stop 8 pt short of the top and 6 pt of the bottom. The preview follows Finder's
layout: the thumbnail, name, kind and size, then a rule and the dates as label and value.

**No sidebar.** A places sidebar was built first and rejected by the user: a sidebar is wasted space most
of the time, and avoiding it is part of why they wanted a Finder replacement. The places went back where
they were, done properly. The first crumb is a pop-up: the place's symbol, its name, and the standard
chevron.up.chevron.down. Its menu has Finder's keys shown (⇧⌘I for iCloud Drive added to Files' keys), the
place holding the folder checked and positioned over the crumb as `NSPopUpButton` places its selection,
then the volumes and Go to Folder…. `Place.holding` picks the nearest place, so Home rather than the disk
it is on; it moved to `Dock2System`, with a test. `Places` is read when Files opens and on volume mount,
unmount and rename, not per render: it asks for every mounted volume.

**The bar.** `PathBar` is a breadcrumb of `PillButton`s, which are custom views rather than borderless
`NSButton`s so they can draw a rounded fill for hover, press, drop target and the current tab. Crumbs and
pills are reused across renders, since Files renders on every selection change and a rebuilt crumb would
drop its hover. A long path folds from the second crumb on into "…", a menu of what it hides. Hover is
routed from `PointerMonitor`: `PanelController.pointerMoved` hit-tests and lights one `Hoverable`. dock2 is
never active, so tracking areas hear only some moves (the label hover change found the same). A label's
`intrinsicContentSize` came back short of its text after a font change ("Ho…", "Modifiec"), so pills and
the preview measure with `sizeThatFits` unbounded.

**Tabs.** `FilesState` keeps up to six, a new one beside the current, closing showing the next or the
previous. `TabStrip` shows only New Tab with one tab, because a single pill would repeat the path. From two
there is a pill each, the current one tinted (the bar's ink at 9%), 84–176 pt,
sharing up to half the bar. Pills take drops, spring open their tab when a drag is held over them, swap
their icon for a close button on hover, and carry the ring that the "other tab's" chip had. ⌘K offers Copy
to and Move to each other tab's folder. Keys: ⌘T, ⌃⇥ / ⌃⇧⇥, ⇧⌘] / ⇧⌘[, ⌘W. A tab change crossfades the
columns over 0.15 s.

**Legibility.** Over a split backdrop (a dark terminal beside a white page), the panel's glass came out
two-toned and small text across the seam lost contrast. Five variants were put side by side in a harness
built from `GlassLayer` and `GlassRecipe`, over the same split behind each: the recipe as it was, the
filter's face fill at 0.6, the fill at 0.75–0.85 over a 24–30 pt blur, a Fiber-style 5 pt glass rim around
a frosted inner glass, and the rim around a solid body. The rims framed the panel in whatever lay behind
it, and the two tones showed through. The fill at 0.8–0.85 over a 24 pt blur read most evenly, but it
stopped feeling like glass; the user chose the fill at 0.6 (light 97% white, dark 11% grey) on the
recipe's own blur, which takes the contrast over the edge and keeps the panel glassy. It applies to the
field, search and Files alike, so nothing changes material mid-grow. A raised white pill disappears on a
frosted body, so the current tab became a tint, and the banner's card went from white to the same ink.

**The path's end** is the only full-colour crumb: the selected item when one shows, otherwise the current
folder. All crumbs share one weight. A bold current folder ahead of a selected item read as out of place.

**The divider** between the field and what grows from it never showed. It was a bare `CALayer` on the
panel's view, which AppKit ordered beneath the glass, and in black at 12% it would have vanished in dark
mode anyway. It is now a view in the clipped container, black 12% in light and white 14% in dark. This
also shows it under search's results, as its code always intended.

Verified on the live dock2 by recording the entrance at 120 fps before and after, and by screenshots in
both appearances: a deep path folding nothing, two tabs, ⌘K's Copy to/Move to, the places pop-up opened by
a click, and hover lit from the monitors; the frosted panel alone and as Files with two tabs, in both
appearances, over the split. `make test` passes, with `FilesState`'s tab test rewritten for
many tabs and a test for `Place.holding`.

## Observations

- On this macOS 27 install, `NSMenu` draws no item images; even a bare test menu's size does not change
  with them. The places menu sets SF Symbols that macOS does not draw here. It reads fine without them.
- Synthetic typing (CGEvent unicode strings, or real key codes) into a text field of the non-activating
  stage did not arrive while Fiber was frontmost, though keys to the columns did. Typing a path and ⌘F's
  query were not checked live with synthetic input; a person typing is unaffected as far as is known.
- The dismissal of a grown panel still snaps it to the field's capsule before sinking, unchanged here.
- With six tabs and a deep path, the crumbs fold to Home, "…" and the last folder; not seen live.
- The banner (progress, conflicts, the access ask) was restyled as a card laid out against its width,
  but no conflict or long copy was run to see it.
- Search's divider now shows; it was not compared against Spotlight's.
