# Shortcuts' tree, laid out as a Form lays out

> Change `1793682576` — 2026-11-02

## Goal

Settings → Shortcuts ([1793678554](1793678554.md)) worked, but did not look like it belonged. Every recorder sat
on a line of its own under its title, and as the window was resized they wrapped there one row at a time, each
at the width its own title allowed. A note or a problem was a row of its own, centred under the row it was
about. The commands sat flush with their node's header, so the tree showed no depth, and an off plugin's
"Off: its shortcuts wait until it is on" was squeezed in beside its name.

## Implementation

Measured in a scratch harness first, as `OBSERVING.md` asks for a look: the section's views copied into a small
SwiftUI app with the same `NavigationSplitView`, grouped `Form` and sidebar width, and captured by window id at
560, 708 and 1000 points wide. The harness drew today's section exactly as dock2 does, wrapping and centred
captions included, so the variants it drew can be trusted.

**The recorder sets its own size and baseline.** Two separate causes, found one after the other:

- An `NSViewRepresentable` without `sizeThatFits` takes whatever width it is offered. In a `Form` row it takes
  the whole row, and `LabeledContent` puts it under the label. `sizeThatFits` now returns 150 × 22 whatever
  the offer, and the row keeps it on the label's line at every width tried.
- The row was still two lines tall, its title high and the recorder low. The grouped `Form` aligns a row by
  first text baseline. SwiftUI takes a representable's baseline as its top edge, which hung the recorder below
  the title's baseline. Overriding `firstBaselineOffsetFromTop` on the `NSView` changed nothing: SwiftUI does not
  read it. `ShortcutRecorder` is now a `View` around the representable. It sets the `.firstTextBaseline` and
  `.lastTextBaseline` guides from the same font metrics `RecorderView` draws its text with, and the text is
  drawn at that baseline.

Fixing the recorder, not each row, also fixes General's summon row, Clipboard's row and the first launch's
recorder. The first launch drops the `.frame(width: 180)` it used to stop the stretching.

**Notes and problems are the label's subtitle.** `LabeledContent`'s label holds the title and, at most, one
more `Text`, which the Form draws under the title in its subtitle style. A row with no subtitle came out
pixel-identical whether the label used a bare `if` or two separate `LabeledContent`s, so the `if` stays.

**A node is a `Section(isExpanded:)`.** On this macOS the grouped Form draws a collapsible section natively:
a chevron, then the header's icon and bold name, then the commands in their own box with separators. Collapsed,
only the header shows. This was chosen over two alternatives the harness drew. A `DisclosureGroup` in one
section leaves the children flush with their header. A `DisclosureGroup` as each section's first row, its
children padded, still lets the rows' sizes vary. A collapsible section takes no footer, so an off plugin says
"Off" at its header's end, and the closing footer adds that an off plugin keeps its shortcuts for when it is
on again.

## Observations

- Seen in the harness only, both the copy of the views and the shipped `ShortcutRecorder.swift` compiled
  against a `KeyChord` stub. The running dock2 could not be replaced from this session, so the real window has
  not been seen: the plated 16-point icons in the headers, the app icon as Dock2's, General's and Clipboard's
  rows, and the first launch's recorder without its frame.
- Seen in dark mode only.
- The recorder still has no visible way to clear it. Delete while it is recording clears it, but nothing says
  so.
