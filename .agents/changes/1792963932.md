# The gem is an emerald

> Change `1792963932` — 2026-10-25

## Goal

The gem was a regular icosahedron, twenty equal triangles tinted cyan, read as a die more than a gem. The user asked for
something more gem-like: an organic shape with faces that are not uniform, coloured as an emerald with some variety of
shade between facets.

## Implementation

- **The stone.** `Stone` holds twelve fixed corners and the twenty triangles of their convex hull, worked out once
  (every three corners with all the others on one side). The corners came from an icosahedron's: each direction turned
  by up to ±0.28 in each axis, its radius varied by ±16%, the whole scaled 0.92 × 1.18 × 0.9, so slightly long, and
  normalised to a radius of 1. A seeded generator in a scratch renderer produced them, and the chosen seed's corners
  are written into the source as a table, so nothing random ships. The facets differ by up to three times in area.
  Keeping twenty triangles leaves the pour's strip and the return as they were. The strip's slot width now follows the
  face count rather than assuming twenty.
- **The colour.** A facet is emerald, hue 0.425 and saturation 0.95, with its own small tint in hue, saturation and
  brightness, a fixed scatter by facet index. It is lit by its turned normal, as before, and glints toward white as it
  turns the light toward the eye (a mirror reflection to the 12th power). Front facets are 96% opaque, so little of
  whatever lies beneath mixes in. Edges are fainter, 0.24 white.
- **The marble lets the colour through.** Measured with emerald swatches under the field's own recipe, the dark panel
  glass lifted 18,45,23 to 45,79,51, through its colour matrix's black of 0.1675, and pressed 183,240,202 down to
  127,189,148, through its luma cap of 0.7475. However deep the facets were drawn, they came out a milky leaf-green:
  under the glass, hue 0.26–0.38, saturation 0.18–0.62, value 0.29–0.76. The marble's lens now also takes the colour
  matrix to black 0.06 and white 0.96, under a luma cap of 0.92. It comes and goes with the lens, so the field's glass is
  untouched. The marble keeps a light tint: its empty interior is 217,195,167 over a backdrop of 233,215,192, where
  fully cleared it was 239,216,186, a bright hole. The facets' hue is drawn at 0.425 to land at 0.36–0.42 on screen.
- **Tuned on the real glass.** The look was set by comparing variants in the app under its glass, in dark and light
  appearance, after an offline renderer chose the shape and a first colour.

## Observations

- Not tested: the emerald's look over a dark window behind the marble. Each appearance was seen only over a light
  backdrop.
- The clearer lens applies in light appearance too, where the panel's own black point is 0.5125. There it moves
  further, and the marble at rest is closer to clear glass than the dark one.
- The first in-app comparisons were void, because dock2 empties its environment at launch and the temporary overrides
  were read from it. `OBSERVING.md` now says so.
- Behaviour is unchanged: the gem tests pass as they stand, and the pour and the return were filmed in emerald.
