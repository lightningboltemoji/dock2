# A scope along the panel's top

> Change `1794367841` — 2026-11-10

## Goal

A plugin's list often holds more than one set of things: shares on this Mac and on servers, one server's of several. The
field filters by text, which cannot say "only the remote ones" or "only nas.local". Plugins needed a menu of their own
options that narrows a list or a grid, and somewhere on the panel to put it that the bar at the foot, already holding the
field and Actions ⌘K, did not have room for.

## Implementation

**The model.** `ScopeNode` on a list or a grid: options in sections, each an id, a title and an icon, and `value`, the
chosen option's id, falling back to the first. The plugin owns its meaning. dock2 draws the menu, shows a choice at once,
and sends `scope` (view, value, id); the plugin answers with the items the option leaves and `value` naming it. Filtering
by item membership in dock2 (items listing the options they belong to) was considered and rejected. It needs a special
"All" option, weighs down every item, and cannot express a server whose items must be fetched. A plugin narrows what it
holds in one call, which answers within the same event, so the round trip costs nothing visible. The event's id comes
from the same counter as `filter`'s, so `answering` drops a slow server's stale answer to an earlier scope or text. It
counts as interactive (the 50 ms budget) but not as an action, so its answer opens nothing. dock2 takes ⌘P for it, as
Raycast's dropdown does, and `PluginChord` now refuses ⌘P to plugins. Only the demo bound it, and it moves to ⇧⌘P.

**Where it stands.** On a bar of bare ground along the panel's top, a row tall like the field's, its pill at the trailing
end mirroring Actions ⌘K at the foot. The person chose that over the leading end. The panel grows by the bar (50 pt past
the 6 pt rim) rather than the pane shrinking. That keeps `product/panel.md`'s promise that a plugin lays out against a
known pane, and a scoped list stands 520 pt tall, as tall as Files, which already fits on screen.

**The motion.** A head that changed outside the motion would make the pane's top jump 50 pt whenever a scoped screen came
or went: the body springs, the inset doesn't. So the bar is part of the panel's motion. `Growth` gains a fifth edge, the
head, stepped on the top edge's spring and in its direction, out or in. When the panel grows by exactly the bar, the two
springs move identically and the pane's top, the clear glass's top less the head (`PanelMetrics.layering`), holds still
through the overshoot. The test runs both ways and checks it to 1e-6. A bar that comes with no growth, such as Files
(also 520 tall) after a scoped view, moves the pane on a critically damped spring. Capping the pane at its resting top
was tried first. It needs the cap kept while shrinking and dropped for a taller unscoped screen, and it still jumps when
popping to search.

`ScreenRide` gains `scope`, the top right corner, and the contents now ride the pane's top rather than the body's, which
is the same thing without a bar. Riding the top edge alone, the pill sat over the pane's first row for the first frames
of a bar opening, which film showed. So its container is masked by a cut the driver plays with the glass
(`ScreenRiders.scopeCut`): above the pane's top while the glass moves, so the pill comes out from behind it as the rows
come out from behind its foot, and above the field at rest, so the open menu can drop over the pane. A scope that
leaves fades out where it stands, its bar rect kept (`scopeBar`), so it doesn't jump to the origin mid-fade.

**One menu, two bars.** `ActionsBubble`'s pill-into-menu machinery became `BarMenu`, parameterised by edge (bottom
opens up, top opens down), its chord (⌘K, ⌘P), and entries: items with an icon, a chord, destructive, or a check;
headings; separators. `ActionsBubble` and `ScopeMenu` are thin subclasses that build entries and say what a choice
does. Two fixes came with it, and Actions gains both:

- The menu stops 15 pt from its room's far edge and scrolls past that. An Actions menu longer than the panel used to
  grow past its top.
- Rows are measured with `sizeThatFits`. A truncating label's `intrinsicContentSize` is 4 pt short (322 against 326 for
  a 47-character name), which cut every widest row's last letters.

A menu closing into a pill whose label changed on the way, as the scope just chosen does, retargets its closing spring
to the new capsule. Before, it shrank to the old pill's width and snapped wide at the end.

**Screens.** `Screen.scope` is a `ScopeOffer` (the node and what choosing does). `NodeScreen` keeps the last choice
(`chosen`) over the node's value until the owner's next update, so the pill says what the person chose while a server
answers, and the field's spinner shows if the plugin marks the list loading. A list or a grid whose chosen option changed
selects its first item, as a new query does.

**The demo.** Scoped to a Server: this Mac and All answered at once, Remote and a server after 450 ms, and ⌘K taking
the scope off the open view and putting it back. Grid in a Scope: warm or cool tints. Forty-Eight Servers: 49 options
in titled sections, one name far wider than the menu's minimum.

## Observations

- Seen in a golden-gate VM in both appearances. The menu opens from ⌘P and from a click on the pill; ↓ steps over
  headings; Esc closes the menu without popping, and a second Esc pops. Choosing works by key and by click. The long
  menu widens to its longest name, stops above the pane's foot and scrolls with the selection. At 60 fps
  (`ScreenCaptureKit`), the push from the gallery raises the panel's top about 42 pt while the pane's top stays within
  a pixel. A pop leaves the pane still, and so does the scope being taken off an open view and put back
  (`PanelController.setScope`), the pill coming out from behind the pane's top.
- Not filmed: Reduce Motion. The scope comes and goes with the growth there, unanimated, as screens do.
- `aWatchHearsChangesUntilItsFolderGoes` failed once under the full suite. It passed three times alone and on the next
  full run. That's FSEvents timing, unrelated to this change.
- dock2's own views could take a scope too: Clipboard History by kind (text, images, files, links) is the obvious one.
  `ViewHost.choose(scope:)` is there for it; none uses it yet.
- The scope has no title of its own. VoiceOver hears a pop-up button named by the chosen option. A plugin with two
  dimensions to choose (server and status) has one menu, sections apart, and one choice.
