# Search: apps, commands, files, picks, the echo

> Change `1792496911` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Roadmap step 4: the app catalog, the commands, the Files source; sections, the top hit, the stability rules,
learned picks; the echo and temporary tiles; actions.

## Implementation

**Core first.** `Matcher`, `SearchSession` and `LearnedPicks` are pure and tested against the product's own
examples ("ss" for Screen Sharing, "vsc" for Visual Studio Code; late results at the far end; a pick
outranking an exact match). The session is a value replaced on every keystroke, so a stale source's answer
is dropped by generation, not by locking.

**Spotlight's rows measured.** From a capture of Spotlight showing "saf": rows on a 56 pt pitch, a 36 pt icon
18 pt in, titles from 72 pt — "Safari" and "safety_hub" ink 41 and 81.5 pt wide, which 16.5 pt regular gives
(41.0, 80.75) — and a selection band 55 pt tall, inset ~10 pt, the glass at 0.905 of its brightness beneath.

**The catalog.** Scanning showed `/Applications/Safari.app` is a symlink to the cryptex with the hidden flag,
which `skipsHiddenFiles` dropped: "saf" found no Safari. Dot-names are skipped by hand instead.

**Files.** `kMDItemPath` conditions are ignored by Spotlight (6,658 hits for "saf" with or without excluding
`*/out/*`), so build trees cannot be filtered in the query; with two Chromium checkouts here, a 400 cap
returned nothing but build output. The cap is 2,000 and ranking sinks `out`, `obj`, `build`, `target`,
`node_modules`, `.git` and other hidden folders. Timing on this index: a query's first run 50–720 ms, then
about 20 ms whether `cd` or `cdw`; typing a word warms its prefixes.

Verified on this Mac: "saf" — Safari as the top hit with Safari's tile wearing the echo, files arriving after
the first frame above the apps without moving them; "sett" — dock2's Settings command first (exact), System
Settings above it, ↑ selecting it and a dimmed temporary tile standing in for it on the dock, ⌘K listing
its actions; files' subtitles truncated in the middle.

## Observations

- Not yet: "Show all…" (views), a path typed in the field (Files), Quick Look, excluded folders, the row
  naming places file search cannot see, Settings → Search.
- Matched ranges are computed but not drawn in bold.
- A cold Files query can take a second; nothing is warmed at launch.
- The panel's glass jumps to each new height rather than growing.
