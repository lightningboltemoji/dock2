# The keyboard: the field, reveal-to-type, ⌘space, the ring

> Change `1792490900` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Roadmap step 3: reveal-to-type with the dwell and the typing guard; the field; the ring; ⌘1–9 and number
badges; the ⌘space takeover through the journal and the guard, tested on the crash path.

## Implementation

`Interaction` (Core, tested) is the reducer for who has the keyboard; `InteractionController` runs it: the
stage made key with the field first responder takes the keyboard, `NSApp.deactivate()` gives it back; the
dwell (150 ms) on a timer; the typing guard from `CGEventSource.secondsSinceLastEventType` (0.5 s);
dismissal on Esc at an empty field, ⌘W, a click elsewhere (global monitor), another app activating, or an
app opened from the dock. `HotKeys` registers chords with Carbon; with `summonShortcut` at ⌘space, Spotlight's
hot key entry 64 is a journaled takeover, engaged before the chord is registered and released live when the
setting changes.

**Measured.** The system Dock's keyboard focus (⌃F3) on macOS 27 is no ring: the icon darkened to 0.48, as
a press darkens it, with its label — the pressed shade is now 52% black, and "the ring" draws that.
Spotlight's field: 640 × 57 circular capsule, a 23 pt magnifier from 22.5 pt, 26 pt regular text from 63 pt
with its baseline 19.5 pt up. Its glass is far lighter than the platter's; a probe fitted the label's recipe
(saturation 1.0612, a sharp rim) and a two-layer shadow standing in for Spotlight's window shadow to 3.96
RMS over the glass, its edges and surroundings (from 10.1). Two recipes cannot merge in one SDF tree, so the
field grows from a sliver at the platter's top edge in its own glass rather than parting from it.

**Stage.** The stage now covers the screen and never resizes: a probe showed moves and scrolls, like clicks,
pass through its transparent pixels, and a resizing stage rebuilt the glass mid-motion (and, resized in
place, drew seams). Glass shapes animate with explicit animations from their presentation values, since
implicit ones skip a layer made in the same transaction.

Verified on this Mac: ⌘space summons and dismisses; a probe editor in front keeps the menu bar, resigns key
while dock2 types, and gets its typing back ("ac"); reveal-to-type and the typing guard; the ring with ← →,
↑ for its menu (opening above the tile); ⌘ held for badges; ⌘1; ⌘, bringing Settings forward
(`activate(ignoringOtherApps:)`, the only call cooperative activation honoured from a non-activating
panel); summoning over a full-screen app; `kill -9` with ⌘space taken — the guard switched entry 64 back on,
Spotlight answered ⌘space again, and `com.apple.dock` matched the backup.

## Observations

- The field's contents fade through `alphaValue`: AppKit owns a layer-backed view's layer opacity and resets
  a value set on it directly, which is why the first recordings showed them at once.
- Under auto-hide the field grows from where the platter is heading, while the platter rises with it.
- Spotlight's text is vibrant (plus-darker, tinted by the glass); dock2's is plain label colour, ~17 levels
  less blue. Dark mode of the panel is unfitted.
