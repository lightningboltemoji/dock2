# The ground comes in on time of its own

> Change `1793763137` — 2026-11-03

## Goal

Glass-on-solid (`17a1ad4`) turned the grown panel into a solid ground with a pane of glass laid on it, but
the switch from the clear field to that ground happened in a single frame as results first grew the panel.
The panel went from clear glass to fully solid, the pane already drawn in. A recording of the summon and the
first letter showed it at 120 fps: frame 36 was the clear field, frame 37 was the finished grown panel.

## Implementation

`PanelMetrics.layering` derived how solid the ground was, the clear glass's fade and the pane's draw-in from
the body's height, over its first 22 pt of growth (smoothstep). The growth spring (0.30 s response, from the
field to some 450 pt of rows) covers 22 pt in about 25 ms, so the crossfade landed in one or two frames however
it was eased. Any ramp measured in distance would compress the same way for a long growth.

The ground's progress is now a spring of its own in `FieldMotion`: critically damped, 0.30 s heading in and
0.22 s heading back to the field (`GrowthMotion.groundInResponse` / `groundOutResponse`), its target 1 whenever
the target body is taller than the field. It is sampled and baked with everything else and carried on
`FormFrame.ground`, so the window server plays it from keyframes like the rest of the motion. A reversal partway
through (a letter typed and deleted) carries on from the speed it has. It runs about level with the growth:
30% at 50 ms, 63% at 100 ms, 92% at 200 ms, whether the panel grows by one row or eight.

`layering(body:radius:fieldHeight:ground:)` now takes that progress. The clear glass is the whole body,
inset by `sheetInset × ground` and fading out. The pane always sits on the field's top and fades in, drawn in
from the body's edge by the same inset. The old split, which slid from the body's top down to the field's
top, would have shown as a wipe through the rows once progress lagged the geometry, so it was dropped.

Two things the one-frame switch had hidden showed once it was gradual:

- **The selection band.** `GroundInset` is the ground's own material, so it sat fully solid in still-clear
  glass for the first ~5 frames. `FormDriver` now hands the ground's keyframes to `RowsView.playGround`, which
  plays them on every row's inset: each one already on screen or spare, and each one made later. Whichever
  row is selected, and whenever, its band is exactly as solid as the ground. Other `RowsView`s, a list
  screen's, never get keyframes, so they stay solid.
- **The form's rest.** `FieldMotion.isField` now also waits for the ground to be gone (< 0.02), so a form
  never starts resting on a part-solid field. In practice the ground clears before the body is within 1 pt
  of the field, so this costs no time.

`setTarget` takes `fading`. `PanelController` passes `hasArrived`, so a screen opened from nothing is solid
from its first frame, as before. Under Reduce Motion the body is there at once and the ground still fades,
as the frost used to.

Observed in release builds with `debug record reach`, over the real desktop and over a still backdrop of
stripes and discs, in dark and light, and with Reduce Motion. Typing `s` moves the frame-against-neighbours
score through a smooth bell over about 14 frames (peak 4.8) where before it was one jump. Esc back to the
field takes about 4 frames to clear. `s` then ⌫ 60 ms later rises a little and settles back with no spike.
Files opened from nothing is solid on its first frame.

## Observations

- A grown panel dismissed still snaps to the field before sinking (`hide`: "A grown panel first becomes the
  field again, at once"). The ground now fades over that sink rather than vanishing with the snap, but the
  snap in shape is still there, and it is the next most abrupt moment in the panel's motion.
- Glass-on-solid itself has not been through the docs pass. `product/visual.md` ("The glass", "Glass and the
  sheet": an 8 pt sheet, the hairline, "one body of glass"), and in `IMPLEMENTATION.md` the sheet's material,
  the hairline between the field and the rows, and the toast "frosted as the grown panel is", still describe
  the frosted design. This change rewrote only the passages about how the grown panel's material comes and
  goes.
- `GlassLayer.setFrosted` is still called (`PanelController.show`) though nothing frosts the panel's glass
  any more. It is left for that pass.
