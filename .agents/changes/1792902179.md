# A caret for the open tile

> Change `1792902179` — 2026-10-24

## Goal

Opened from its tile, Files sat over the dock with "Files" still labelled above the tile, because the pointer
was still there. The label named the tile but not the fact that the panel above was its own.

## Implementation

- **The open tile.** A screen names the dock tile it belongs to (`Screen.tile`, nil by default; Files' is
  `.finder`). `ViewsController.onTop` reports the screen on top whenever it changes: pushed, shown again
  below a pop, or gone back to the search or dismissed. `AppController` passes that screen's tile to
  `DockController.setOpen`. While a tile is open, its label is not shown, from hover or from the ring, and a
  caret stands in its place.
- **The caret** (`CaretView`) is a 30 × 18 pt capsule of the label's glass, centred where the label's capsule
  would be, with a chevron 10 × 3 pt drawn over it in the label's ink at 2 pt. It fades in over 0.15 s and out
  over 0.12 s. It is placed again after every relayout, so it follows its tile, and it goes with the dock's
  slide away and comes back with its slide in.
- **A rejected caret**: a chevron of glass alone, two turned capsules in the label's recipe. Turned long and
  thin, the SDF glass drew them mirrored top to bottom, offset about 40 pt, and cut off at a line through the
  point. The label's arrow, a square turned 45°, looks the same either way up, so it never showed. Pure glass
  also gives a bare chevron no contrast against a light wallpaper. The capsule carries the chevron the way the
  label carries its name.

## Observations

- Plugin views take no tile yet: a view a plugin's tile opens could name `.plugin(key)`, but the tile's click
  goes to the plugin, which pushes its view through the context without saying where it came from.
- Seen in screenshots of the debug build: the caret over Finder's tile, no label on hover while open, and the
  caret gone on dismissal.
- Pills for the path bar's folders were tried alongside this and taken out to be thought through: filling
  every folder at rest makes the path's last pill and the current tab's look alike at the bar's two ends.
