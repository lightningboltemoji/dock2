# A minimised window arrives in its shape

> Change `1792806967` — 2026-10-23

## Goal

Minimising a window that was far from square — a skinny one, a wide one — first showed its tile as a square
blank window with a bare corner, which then turned into the window's shape with its app's icon about half a
second later.

## Implementation

**Measured first**, with a test app's 260 × 820 window, the debug build's dock held up, the display recorded at
120 fps, and an `AXObserver` of its own on the app and the Dock, polling the window's attributes every 5 ms:

- `miniaturize(_:)` at 0. dock2's tile at +37 ms — a square, no icon.
- At +532 ms, in the same millisecond: the app's `AXWindowMiniaturized`, its window's `AXMinimized` turning
  true (false at every poll before), and the Dock's `AXCreated`. The tile crossfaded to the window's shape.

The Dock lists its item from the start of the genie but posts `AXCreated` only at the end. dock2 found the item
early through the badge read, which runs four times a second while the dock is on screen and once as it
starts to show. The app says nothing of the window until the genie ends, so the item, which has only a
title, had no window to match, and `WindowTileImage` drew its placeholder: a square window with a bare corner.
With auto-hide on, this shows when the dock is up during a minimise or is revealed within half a second of
one. Since `AXCreated` and the app's notice reach two threads, each publishing to the main thread separately,
an item could also be drawn unmatched for a frame without any early read. That case was not measured.

**An item is not shown until it is matched.** `MinimisedWindowMatch` keeps when each unmatched item began
waiting. `isShown` is true once the item is matched, or once it has waited `patience` (2 s) for a window that
is not coming, as for a window retitled mid-genie or an app dock2 does not watch. Then it is drawn as the
placeholder, so the dock still has every item the Dock has. `DockController` filters `windowTiles` by it and
runs the match again at `nextDeadline(after:)`, a `DispatchWorkItem` like the spring-loading one.
`nextDeadline` looks only past `now`, so an item already due does not reschedule itself. An item that loses
its match waits afresh, so a window the app says is restored now leaves before the Dock lets its item go,
where it used to turn into the placeholder for those few milliseconds.

**An item's title is taken again at each read.** A window retitled 0.1 s into its genie showed that the Dock
takes up the new title. In one run its item read the new title at `AXCreated`. In another it read the old one
there and the new one afterwards. `SystemDockItems` kept the title of its first read, often a mid-genie one, so
the item kept the old label and never matched. It now takes the Dock's title at every read and keeps the item's
id. An existing match is undisturbed, since the match is by id, and an item still waiting matches at the next
read under its new title.

The rejected alternative was to match an unmatched item early to the window of that title not yet minimised,
which would show the right shape from the genie's start. `AppWindows` does not follow resizes, so it would need
every app's resize notifications or a read on demand. A title shared by two windows would also make it guess,
and a wrong guess shows the wrong shape and then corrects it, which is this bug again. The genie flies to the
hidden system Dock, not to dock2, so a tile that arrives as the genie ends loses nothing.

Verified live with the debug build, the dock held up, recorded and polled: the skinny window's tile appeared at
+554 ms, skinny with its icon from its first frame. A 1100 × 240 window was wide from its first frame. A restore
shrank the skinny tile out with no square; the app's `AXWindowDeminiaturized` came 19 ms before the Dock's
`AXUIElementDestroyed`, and the tile left between them. A window retitled 0.1 s into its genie never matched,
and its tile appeared as the placeholder 2.06 s after the minimise. That run predates reading the title again.
`make test` passes, with four new `MinimisedWindowMatch` tests, one of them a waiting item matching by its new
title.

## Observations

- Reading the title again is unit-tested in `MinimisedWindowMatch` but not yet seen live. Its one live run
  went to another session's debug dock2, built without it, because the instance lock refused this one. That
  run still fell back to the placeholder at 2.03 s.
- With the dock hidden, nothing reads the Dock between an item's `AXCreated` and the dock's next showing. A
  title the Dock takes up late is read at that showing, possibly after the item has waited out its patience
  as an unseen placeholder. The read as the dock starts to show comes before the slide.
- A genie slower than 2 s (Shift-minimise's slow motion) shows the placeholder before the match, as before.
- The one-frame race between the two threads at the genie's end is argued, not recorded: dock2 hidden during
  the genie draws nothing anyone sees.
- This Mac re-tiles a new window shortly after `setFrame`. Pinning `minSize` and `maxSize` held the test
  windows' shapes, which made a landscape window testable for the first time.
