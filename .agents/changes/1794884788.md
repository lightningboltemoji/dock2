# Files' Quick Look follows the selection

> Change `1794884788` — 2026-11-16

## Goal

Space in Files laid a Quick Look card over the columns and never changed it. The columns keep the keys under the
card, so ↓ moved the selection, and the path bar with it, while the card stayed on the item Space was pressed on.
Finder's Quick Look follows its selection.

Finder was measured in the `golden-gate-base` VM (macOS 27.2), column view, over `file1.txt`…`file5.txt` and a folder
`sub` holding `inner.txt`, with Quick Look open:

| Keys | Finder's Quick Look shows |
| --- | --- |
| ↓ from file1, then ↓ | file2, then file3 |
| ↓ onto `sub` | a card for the folder: its name, size, item count, modified date |
| → into `sub` | `inner.txt`, its first item |
| ← out of `sub` | `sub` |
| ⇧↓ from file1 | file2, the item gained, with ‹ › to step through both |
| ⇧↑ from file5 | file4 |
| ⇧↓ ⇧↓ from file1, then ⇧↑ | file3, then file2 |
| ⇧↑ ⇧↑ from `sub` (file4…`sub`), then ⇧↓ | file4, then `sub`: the last selected, not the end that moved |
| ⌥⌘A, nothing selected | the folder in view, `QLTest` |

## Implementation

- **`FilesState.quickLook(_:from:to:)`**: the item the selection gained, else the shown item while it stays selected,
  else the last selected. It is pure and sits beside `successor`, Finder's other selection rule, with a test of the
  table above. The two shrink cases rule out "the end that moved". "The last selected" fits both of them and is the
  simplest rule that does.
- **`FilesScreen`** keeps the card with the selection it last followed. `render`, which every selection change goes
  through (arrows, ← and → between columns, `enter`'s first item, a trash's successor, a tab switch, search's list),
  calls `followQuickLook`. That does nothing while the selection matches the one followed, so the many renders a
  folder watch or a job makes don't reload the preview. With nothing selected it shows `tab.folder`. It closes the
  card in a search with nothing selected, and where the item is not a file URL (a server, a share not mounted), as
  Space refuses one there.
- Comparing against the selection followed, not the item shown, keeps a right-clicked Quick Look on an item outside
  the selection until the selection moves. Comparing against the item shown would put the card back on the
  selection at the next render.
- **`QuickLookCard.show(_:)`** sets the `QLPreviewView`'s item, and does nothing for the item already shown.

## Observations

- Verified in the same VM, on a debug build driven through `dock2 debug key`: ↓ ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑, → into `sub` and ← ← out to
  `QLTest`, ⇧↓ ⇧↓ ⇧↑, ⌘⌫ on file3 (the card moved to file4, the successor) and ⌘Z (back to file3), a right-clicked Quick Look on
  file1 with file3 selected holding through two writes into the folder and then following ↓ to file4, and ⌘F's
  results under ⌘Y. Space and Esc still close it. The baseline build showed the bug on the same path.
- A multi-selection's card shows one item and has no ‹ › to step through the rest, as Finder's has. Space on a
  multi-selection still opens on the first item. Which item Finder opens on was not measured.
- `QLPreviewView` draws a folder as its icon alone. Finder's panel adds the name, size, item count and date.
- Files has no ⌥⌘A. Nothing is left selected when the list's last item goes under the card: ⌘⌫ on file5 in the VM
  put the card on `QLTest`.
