# Tahoe's glass lets clicks through

> Change `1793935277` — 2026-11-05

## Goal

On Tahoe dock2 caught clicks meant for the windows beneath it, at rest, wherever the stage reached, and a click elsewhere
never dismissed it (`1793933814`). `GlassLayer` is the size of the stage, and a backdrop is held to its shapes only by
`sourceSublayerDefinesBounds`, which Tahoe lacks, so there each backdrop filled the screen and took the mouse across it.

## Implementation

Where the key is missing, `GlassLayer` keeps the backdrop's bounds to the shapes (`keepsExtent`), set in the glass's own
coordinates so the source within doesn't move. It branches on the key and not on a version, and macOS 27 takes none of it,
because the key gives it a hit area that follows the shape, which a rectangle would lose. One path for both was rejected for
that reason: the simpler code would have cost the macOS with the better answer.

A shape in motion can't be followed exactly, so the extent covers the whole of a motion before it starts and comes back to
the shapes when it ends, on the transaction's completion. Keyframes say where a shape will go. On a spring it is both ends and
the first swing, which `Motion.overshoot` works out from the damping ratio; no curve dock2 uses leaves 0…1. A count of changes
stops a motion's end from shrinking what a later motion set. `layoutSublayers` now compares `bounds` with what the tree was
built at, as a backdrop's frame no longer equals `bounds` here and would have rebuilt the tree on every layout.

On macOS 27 the layer trees are byte-identical across the change, all 4,238 lines. On a Tahoe 26.6.2 VM a grid of 15 clicks
matches macOS 27 exactly, 12 reaching a window beneath, the hidden field's place among them; the 3 that don't, 6 pt outside the
platter, are caught on both. A click elsewhere dismissed 5 of 5, from none. The extent came back after Files, after a search,
and after motion interrupted twice over. Dock, search and Files at rest match the unfixed build, the panes but for a hairline
along their edges (at most 382 pixels, off by at most 16). Stills through Files growing, a tile arriving, and the sideline's
springs showed no clipping.

## Observations

On Tahoe a backdrop's hit area is a rectangle, 2 pt past its shapes, so a rounded corner or the gap between the platter and the
puck takes a click macOS 27 would pass, and for the length of a motion so does the whole of its way. A looping form holds its
extent until it stops. Only 26.6.2 was run, in a VM, with synthetic clicks. Stills caught a motion every few frames, and an
interrupted spring was checked for where it ended, not looked at as it moved. Reduce Transparency hides the glass, so it never
had the fault. Tahoe's recipes remain unfitted (§16).
