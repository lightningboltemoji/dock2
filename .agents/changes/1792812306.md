# Minimised windows' pictures, on a match that holds

> Change `1792812306` — 2026-10-23

## Goal

The dock drew a minimised window as a blank window in its shape, where the system Dock shows the window
itself. Pictures had been left out to avoid a Screen Recording prompt, not for want of a way to take them. The
shape already rested on matching each of the Dock's items, which carry only a title, to an app window, and
pressure-testing that match found two ways it took the wrong window: a group's hidden tab, still minimised,
taken by a later window of its title; and windows of one title matched in one update, which swapped. A wrong
shape at 64 pt passes unnoticed; a wrong window's contents do not, so the match was made sound before the
pictures went on it.

## Implementation

**Measured first**, with test apps and Terminal, under the user's own auto-hidden Dock and again under dock2's
takeover; every probe is a scratchpad program, recorded in `OBSERVING.md`'s methods.

- **Order.** Four windows of one app minimised together (Minimize All): the Dock's item order matched the order
  the app's `AXWindowMiniaturized` arrived in 12 of 12 runs, and Terminal's Minimize All, four windows titled
  "tanner — -zsh — 120×30", 5 of 5 — one app's loop drives both. Four apps minimising within 0.1 ms of each
  other agreed 7 of 12 times, adjacent pairs swapped otherwise; 5 ms apart 6 of 8, 20 ms 8 of 9, 50 ms 8 of 8.
  The app says a window is minimised as its genie ends, and the end varies 20–50 ms between apps.
- **Timing.** For a window minimised by its button, by `performMiniaturize` or through `AXMinimized`, app
  active or not, the Dock lists the item and posts `AXCreated` together as the genie ends, about 540 ms in,
  within about 2 ms of the app, either first — so `IMPLEMENTATION.md`'s "lists an item as the genie starts, but
  posts `AXCreated` only as it ends" was not what this macOS does, under the takeover or not, and was
  replaced. Minimize All lists its items as the genies start, about 50 ms in, half a second before the app.
- **Tabs.** A group minimised — by its button, in-app or through Accessibility — reported its hidden tabs
  first and the tab on show last in all 21 runs. Before the minimise, `kAXWindows` lists only the tab on show; once minimised, every tab; after a
  restore or a tab switch, only the tab on show again. The tab on show has an `AXTabGroup` child; each tab's
  `AXWindow` is the group's window, not the tab's own. When the group is the main window, `AXMainWindowChanged`
  arrives mid-burst — after the hidden tabs, before the tab on show — and `kAXWindows` then lists every tab. A
  tab switch posts `AXWindowResized` on the tab coming on show, sometimes nothing else.
- **Capture.** Of a minimised window: `SCScreenshotManager` fails (SCStreamError −3811, every configuration),
  an `SCStream` starts and sends no frame, `CGWindowListCreateImage` (resolved at runtime; gone from the SDK)
  returns nil, and SkyLight's `SLSHWCaptureWindowList` returns the window — its contents current, its own rounded
  corners, no shadow — in about 10 ms. ScreenCaptureKit's window ids are the ones `_AXUIElementGetWindow`
  gives for the app's Accessibility windows. Yesterday's note that ScreenCaptureKit captured minimised windows
  did not reproduce on the same macOS build.
- **The Dock's tile**, photographed with auto-hide off for the purpose (`com.apple.dock` exported first and
  identical after): the window's contents in the 15/16 frame `DockMetrics.windowFrame` already gives, no
  shadow, no outline (the one soft edge pixel is antialiasing). Redrawn while minimised, the window changed in
  a capture and not on the Dock: its picture is taken once.

**The match.** Each item, in the Dock's order, takes the window of its title that no other item has, minimised
within two seconds of the item's first showing — the earliest such. Earliest, in the Dock's order, is the
measured order; the old rule, each item taking the newest, was right only while windows arrived one at a time,
and swapped any that arrived together. The two-second window keeps out a window minimised long before.
`MinimisedWindowMatch.Window` gained `minimisedAt`, and its caller passes only windows that can have an item.

**Hidden tabs.** `AppWindows` keeps the windows the app's list says are on show, read again on a window
created, the main window changing, an unlisted window resized, a window restored and the Space changing; a
window minimised that was not on show is `isHiddenTab`, and the match never sees it. A minimised window keeps
the place the last reading gave it, so a reading mid-burst — the main window changing — leaves the hidden tabs
out and the tab on show in. Rejected: a settle period before matching, which Minimize All defeats (its hidden
tabs and its other windows arrive together); excluding the windows before one with an `AXTabGroup`, which
Safari's in-window tabs also have; and marking a tab hidden when it is first heard at its minimise, which
fails once the group has been minimised and restored, its tabs then known.

**Pictures.** `WindowCapture` holds the private calls — `SLSHWCaptureWindowList` and `SLSMainConnectionID`
from SkyLight, `_AXUIElementGetWindow` from HIServices — reached by name and checked, and refuses without
Screen Recording (`CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess`). The charter's private-API list grew a fourth entry; no
public API takes the picture, and without it the tile is the blank window it was. The picture is taken once,
when the item is matched, on a serial queue, and scaled to 240 pixels at 2× — the largest tile's window — so a
tile size change needs none taken again. The tile waits for it up to 0.25 s; a late one still lands.
`WindowTileImage` draws it in the window's frame with no outline; the blank window is unchanged. The first item
matched without Screen Recording asks for it once (`askedToRecordScreen`), as the first paste asks for
Accessibility; Settings → Permissions shows it with its pane (`Privacy_ScreenCapture`). `debug state` says how
each window tile is drawn.

**Run live** under `DOCK2_DEBUG=1`, each tile's colour read off the screen and each item's window found by
pressing the Dock's own item:

- a lone window: `picture`, its colour;
- a group of three tabs all titled "Same", made the main window and minimised: the tab on show, 5 of 5;
- then a lone window titled "Same", 1.2 s later — inside the two-second window, so this is the hidden-tab
  flag's test: its own picture, 5 of 5, in the Dock's order;
- the red tab put on show, then minimised: red, 3 of 3; restored through the Dock, the blue tab put on show,
  minimised again: blue, 3 of 3;
- Terminal's Minimize All, four windows of one title in four colours: every tile the window the Dock's item
  restores, 5 of 5;
- a tile arrives with its picture 26–57 ms after the app says the window is minimised, polling included.

A test app restoring its own windows after Minimize All looked at first like dock2's doing; with dock2
stopped the test app did it too. Terminal did it once under dock2, in the run just after a Minimize All
pressed while it was not frontmost, and in none of the 13 runs since.

## Observations

- The Dock's picture is taken as the genie starts, the window still active, its buttons in colour; dock2's as
  it ends, the window inactive, its buttons grey.
- At the first minimised window, macOS's Screen Recording prompt appears without a sentence from dock2: the
  dock has nowhere to say why, as `product/README.md`'s principle 8 asks. Settings → Permissions says it.
- Granting Screen Recording while dock2 runs is untested, and so is running without it: this terminal holds the
  grant the debug build inherits. If macOS wants dock2 reopened, reopening restarts the Dock, which brings every
  minimised window back.
- Windows already minimised when dock2 starts give no notification, so a group's hidden tabs among them are not
  told apart. dock2's launch restarts the Dock, which restores them, so this needs a start without one.
- Two apps' windows of one title minimised within about 50 ms of each other can swap.
- A window minimised on another Space without the Space changing — by a script — is taken for a hidden tab:
  its tile is a bare square after two seconds.
- `RunningApps` does not hear an app's activation policy change, so an app that becomes regular after it
  launches may go unwatched, its tiles bare squares. Found with a bare test executable; older than this change.
- `AppWindows` keeps a group's hidden tabs after the group is restored, and the app's menu lists them; older
  than this change.
- An app that takes more than two seconds to say its window is minimised — `AppWindows`' thread held up by
  another app's hang, a second per call — leaves the item unmatched.
- A tile click restoring the window, and `minimizeToApp`, were read in the code, not run.
