# Files opens inside its glass

> Change `1792895169` — 2026-10-24

## Goal

Opening Files, its contents and the lava lamp's droplets both showed outside the glass, or past it, while it grew.

The contents: the screen lies in a container clipped to the glass, at its final layout, and faded in on a guess:
from a quarter of the slide (60 ms) to its end (240 ms). The glass's sides reach their places later than that. On
the panel's own springs they first get there at 0.19 s. From a form, whose ends pour out critically damped
(response 0.42), they creep to within 5 pt of their places only at about 0.41 s. Filmed at about 40 fps from the
lava lamp, the glass's edge cut through the house and "Home" for four frames ("e ⌃⌄", "ome ⌃⌄", a bare "Home"),
and the "+" stood half cut at the right edge, before either sat at its inset. The column's names were cut the
same way. This is the magnifier's problem from `1792869276`, seen in a screen instead of the field.

The droplets: opened from the lava lamp, its two droplets hung off the growing glass's sides for more than six
frames at 25 ms, far past its ends, where a pour melts them in within 0.1 s.

## Implementation

- **The reveal follows the glass.** `FormFrame.holds(_:)` (Core) says whether the body holds the body the panel
  asked for: every edge at its place, past it, or within `GrowthMotion.holdTolerance`. At each change `FormDriver`
  finds the first sample of the track that holds its target. It fades a screen's contents in from there, over
  `GrowthMotion.contentFadeIn` (0.12 s, as a row's fade-in). The fade is the driver's, beside the clip it
  follows. A change before the fade begins moves it with the glass, and one already begun plays on. A new
  screen, the results coming back, and `stop()` call off a fade still to come, so the divider, which shares the
  container, never waits on one.
- **The tolerance is 5 pt**, half the 10 pt a screen keeps its contents in, so the glass is past anything a
  screen draws by at least that much when the fade begins. Files' house icon and column icons sit about 24 pt in
  from the sides and its first row about 16 pt below the top, so in practice the margin is about 19 pt. Waiting
  for 0.5 pt would mean waiting out a pour's last creep: 0.54–0.6 s from a form. First held, growing to Files:
  0.18 s from the capsule or a poured field. From the drop, gem or lava lamp at rest: 0.39 s within 6 pt, 0.43 s
  within 4 pt, and in between at 5 pt. Fully shown 0.12 s after that.
- **The form steps against the band it is drawn on.** `FieldMotion` gave its form the band the panel was
  growing to (`FormSpace.band`, set on each `setTarget`), but drew the form on the band where the growth had it.
  The two agree except while the panel's width moves, as it does growing to Files' 960 pt. The lava lamp places
  its droplets from the band's middle, at the body's ends as measured on the band it was given, so they rode the
  ends Files would have, up to 160 pt past the ends drawn, until the growth caught up. `FieldMotion.space` is
  now derived from the growth at every step, so each form steps, pours, rests and is drawn against one band. The
  drop and gem keep their ends as offsets from the band's edges, so they were drawn right already. The text and
  magnifier, carried from the body's left end, had the same latent mismatch and are carried from the drawn end
  too.
- **Tests.** `onceTheGlassHoldsAScreenItKeepsHoldingIt` checks, for the capsule and each form, from rest and
  poured, at eight moments of the rest, that the body goes on holding Files once it first does. That is what
  lets the fade begin there. It also checks that the glass gets there in time: before 0.235 s on the panel's
  springs, before 0.45 s from a form's rest. `itsDropletsMeltInAsItWidensToAScreen` checks that from 0.1 s no
  form's droplet stands out of the body growing to Files. Run against the band as it was, it fails for the
  lava lamp.
- **Two versions of the reveal were rejected.** Carrying the bar's two ends on the glass's ends, as the field
  carries its magnifier, would leave the columns above still cut by the clip. Making them ride too would slide a
  940 pt layout sideways as the glass opens. Clipping the container 10 pt inside the glass rather than at it
  would keep the corners clear but still cut glyphs, just further in.
- Opened from nothing, the panel's entrance rises and scales the glass in as it grows, so the glass holds Files
  only once risen. The contents now come about 0.36 s after the glass first shows. Before, they stood still
  while the rising glass swept over them. Under Reduce Motion nothing is revealed: the panel crossfades and the
  contents show at once, as before.

## Observations

- The contents come later than they did: fully shown about 0.53 s after opening from a form or from nothing,
  and 0.30 s from the field, against a fade that began at 60 ms. If that reads slow, the lever is a form's pour
  creeping over its last points on a 960 pt target, not the tolerance.
- Search's rows are untouched. They are revealed from the field up as the glass covers them, through a mask of
  their own.
- Filmed before and after: from the lava lamp, typed as a path ("~/"), with Esc back, from nothing, and under
  Reduce Motion, and the droplets at up to 120 fps. A view, the screen a plugin opens, takes the same path but
  was not filmed.
