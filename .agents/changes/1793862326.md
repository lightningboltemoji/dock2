# A section's title is its "Show all"

> Change `1793862326` — 2026-11-04

## Goal

A section with more than it showed had two rows at its head: a title that could not be selected, and directly under it a
selectable "Show all 8 apps…" band, repeating the title's word and spending 40 pt on it. The two are one idea, so they
are now one row: `Apps ………… Show all 8`.

## Implementation

`SearchSession.Row` loses `.showAll`: `.title` carries `all: Int?`, and a title with a count is selectable. One case rather
than a `.showAll` standing in for the title, because a section can pass its share after the first frame, and a change of
id would fade the heading out and in where it stands. `title:` stays its id throughout. ↑ takes as many presses as it
did, since the two rows were neighbours.

The words go at the far end rather than straight after the title. Both were captured on the real panel. Inline, "Apps
Show all 8" reads as one phrase, and selected it leaves ↩ mid-row with the band empty beyond it. At the far end ↩ keeps
the column of `Open ↩` as ↑ moves through the list, and a tall result list gets one clean right edge.

The words are `accessory`, but ↩ is a field of its own (`returnMark`) rather than part of the hint as on other rows. In
one right-aligned string, ↩ arriving pushed the words left by its width, a shift under the eye with nothing else moving.
So they stop short of the margin by `returnRoom`, selected or not, and only ↩ comes and goes. The cost is that at rest
they sit 15 pt in from the margin, about where the word in `Open ↩` ends.

The title's row stays 32 pt, so a section without more is where it was. The band fills the 24 pt beside its section, not
the row, because a full-height band would need the title centred in 32 pt, halfway between the section it heads and the
one above. `RowView.line` is that height, for the band and for centring both ends of the row. The band is a capsule at it.

One click still selects and two open. Opening on one click would read better on words that look like a link, but the
second click of a habitual double-click would land on the list that just opened, on whatever row is under the pointer.

The count is now what the list holds: it left out the top hit, so "Show all 52 apps…" opened 53. Counts are grouped, and
dropping the noun ends "Show all 28 clipboard…". The feature's name loses its ellipsis with the label. VoiceOver's
announcement read title and subtitle alone, which would have said only "Apps", so it and the row's label now share
`RowModel.spoken`; a selected result also says its accessory, as its label did.

## Observations

Checked on the live panel, light and dark: the words' ink at rest and selected, which starts on the same pixel, ↩ ending on the same pixel as `Run ↩`, ↩ on the heading, Esc back with it still selected, one click and two, Files'
heading opening Files, and a view's plain titles. A title sits 0.5 pt higher than it did, centred now. VoiceOver itself
was not listened to.

`debug state` gives such a row's title alone, "Apps", so its count shows only in a capture.

Esc from Files' "Show all" closes Files' search first, leaving Files at home, and a second Esc returns to the query. That
was not changed here, and `IMPLEMENTATION.md` §9's "Esc goes back to it" glosses over the step.
