# Plugins: the engine, the protocol, the SDK, the host, Now Playing, Bitwarden

> Change `1792504326` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

Roadmap step 9: plugins as WebAssembly actors — the engine, the protocol, a Rust SDK, capabilities, views,
results, commands and tiles — and the two first-party plugins, Now Playing and Bitwarden.

## Implementation

**The engine from the spike.** The wasmtime spike's shim became `Engine/`, trimmed to Pulley (the spike:
the Cranelift JIT was killed under the hardened runtime without `allow-unsigned-executable-memory`; Pulley
ran in every configuration), epoch interruption with a wall-clock deadline, and a memory cap. SwiftPM cannot
run cargo, so `make engine` builds the staticlib and wraps it as an XCFramework for a binary target; an
unchanged engine costs a moment, a cold build 76 s.

**An ABI bug the tests found.** The first instance trapped on its first event: the SDK handed out its answer
with `Vec::leak`, whose capacity may exceed its length, and `dock2_free` freed it at its length. A boxed
slice is exactly its length.

**Survival, tested.** Against `Plugins/test-guest`: a loop stopped within its budget and the next event
served by a fresh instance; an 8 MiB-at-a-time hoard stopped at the cap; a panic survived; five traps in a
minute switching the plugin off; the compiled module cached and read back; a capability not granted
refused back to the plugin; storage outliving its instance.

**Key derivation moved to dock2.** Writing Bitwarden showed its KDFs could not run in the plugin: 600,000
PBKDF2 rounds in the interpreter take seconds against a 1 s budget, and Argon2id's 64 MiB is the whole
memory cap. The engine's crate derives natively (PBKDF2 matching Python's `hashlib`; Argon2id at 64 MiB in
94 ms) behind a `derive` effect the plugin asks for.

**Now Playing**, verified with the spike's silent player: the tile showed its artwork and a ring; a click
reached the player as togglePlayPause and the tile said "Paused"; ⌘⇧N in the view reached it as nextTrack
and the view and the tile followed to Track 2. The hover label first never appeared: every tile update
relaid the dock out, which hides the label; a tile now redraws its image alone.

**Bitwarden** is tested natively, not against its servers: its crypto against vectors made by Python and
openssl (`vectors/make.py`) and RFC 6238; the API's parsing in both cases; and the whole flow driven
through events — prelogin, the derivation dock2 would do, a grant answered with a user key sealed under the
stretched master key, a sync of items sealed with it and with an item's own key, a concealed copy, search,
lock, a wrong password refused, unlock from the last sync, a second factor and a new device's code. The
sign-in form was seen on screen and closed with Esc; it was not submitted, to send no made-up credentials
to Bitwarden.

Settings gained Plugins: each plugin's switch, its grants in plain words, results, tiles and settings, and
install from a folder after an alert listing what it would reach.

## Observations

- Bitwarden against bitwarden.com, a self-hosted server, and organizations' items are all untried.
- The test guest lives in the workspace and is routed to `.build/test-plugins` by its id, so it never ships.
- Detail views drew their Markdown in one weight; they now keep emphasis, and show their actions as buttons.
- A plugin installed while dock2 runs joins search at the next launch.
