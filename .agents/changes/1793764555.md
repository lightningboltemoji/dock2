# Glass on solid, written down

> Change `1793764555` — 2026-11-03

## Goal

Glass-on-solid (`17a1ad4`) replaced the frosted panel, a single body of glass with an 8 pt sheet for screens
and a hairline over search's rows, with a solid ground and a pane of glass laid on it. It never had a docs
pass. `product/visual.md`, the glossary and four links still described the sheet. `IMPLEMENTATION.md` still
described the hairline, the 8 pt sheet of `.contentBackground`, the toast "frosted as the grown panel is", and
Files' accent-coloured selection. The frost mechanism was dead code: `PanelController.show` called
`setFrosted(false)` on a glass nothing frosts. Change `1793763137` listed these items and left them.

## Implementation

The frost is gone: `GlassRecipe.frost`, `GlassLayer.setFrosted`, `isFrosted`, `fill` and `fillKey`,
`GlassView.setFrosted`, and the call. Without it, the field's recipe takes the platter base's fill, white at
alpha 0, where it had the frost's colour at alpha 0, and the pane's fill goes from black at alpha 0 to the same.
The glass ignores a fill's colour at alpha 0. Captured over a still backdrop before and after, dark and light,
the grown search panel, a view, a toast and Files were identical to within 2/255. The only differences were the
caret's blink and the lava lamp's droplets moving.

One behaviour changed. Under Increase Contrast, `GlassView` borders the pane, but the panel's outline is now
the ground's, and the ground's rim stayed its faint hairline. Before glass on solid, the whole panel's glass
was bordered. `PanelGround` now draws its rim in the secondary label colour under Increase Contrast, as a glass's
border is, and redraws when the display settings change. Captured with `DOCK2_INCREASE_CONTRAST=1`, both the
outline and the pane are bordered for search and Files, and a toast's capsule is bordered too. Without the
setting, nothing changed.

The docs describe glass on solid as captured in both appearances and under Reduce Transparency and Increase
Contrast. `product/visual.md`'s "Glass and the sheet" is now "Glass on solid": the ground, the field's bare
strip, the pane 6 pt in with its shade as the seam, the selection let down to the ground, and the toast as a
piece of the grown panel. "The sheet" in the glossary is now "the ground, the pane", and the links from
`panel.md`, `plugins/views.md` and `files/README.md` follow it. In `IMPLEMENTATION.md`, §6 gains the ground's
and the pane's numbers and loses the hairline. §7 describes `GroundInset`, §8 describes the sheet as the pane's
frame with nothing drawn of its own and the toast as a small `PanelGround` with a pane, and §9 describes Files'
selection let down to the ground. §3 notes a recipe without a face.

The code now uses the product's words. `PanelMetrics.sheetInset`, `sheetRadius` and `sheet(over:extent:)` are
`paneInset`, `paneRadius` and `pane(over:extent:)`. `PanelSheet` is `ScreenSlot`: it holds a screen's view in the
pane's frame and corners, and draws nothing of its own. Comments that said "sheet" for the pane now say "pane" or
"slot". `FilesLayout`'s banner was "a pane on the sheet" and is now a card, which is what `IMPLEMENTATION.md` called it.
"The sheet" is left to the plugin permissions sheet in Settings, which is a real sheet.

A toast floats `Feedback.gap` above the panel, which is `paneInset`, 6 pt: as far as the pane sits inside the ground.
`IMPLEMENTATION.md` had said 8 pt, from when the sheet's inset was 8.

## Observations

- The rename was built, not captured. It changes no value, and the captures above ran on the build before it.
- Under Reduce Transparency in dark, the pane and the ground fill to the same colour, so the pane's edge
  disappears. A selection still reads. Under Reduce Transparency in light, the ground's shade keeps them apart.
