# Pills

> Change `1794451902` — 2026-11-11

## Goal

A list of short items — symbols, labels, languages, places — spends a 56 pt row on a word or two, and shows seven
of them at a time. Plugins needed a denser layout for such items that keeps everything a list does.

## Implementation

`layout: flow` on a list, not a node of its own: sections, filtering, actions, scope, `selected`, the empty
state and the preview are the list's, unchanged. Only what draws the items differs. `ListComponent` holds a
`RowsView` and a `FlowView` behind one protocol, `ItemsView` (show, select, the clicks, the metrics), and draws
with whichever the layout names. Its selection, pending choice and actions don't know which.

The geometry is Core's (`FlowLayout`), tested: titles on lines of their own, pills read like words between the
rows' margins, a line ending where the next would not fit, and `vertical`, the pill nearest across on the next
line up or down, for ↑ ↓. Pills are 28 pt high and 8 pt apart, a 13 pt title with a 16 pt icon, capped at 240 pt
as tags are. A section's title stands as a row's does, and a gap separates it from the pills before it.

`FlowView` draws everything in sight in one canvas rather than a view per pill, since a flow is for many small
things. The selection is one `GroundInset` moved under the canvas. Widths are measured once per title and kept.
An icon is asked for as its pill first comes into sight, and keeps its room if it draws nothing, as a row's
does.

The first look gave unselected pills a quiet fill (`quaternarySystemFill`). In a capture the selected pill's
well was hard to tell from it, since both are a slightly darker capsule. Unselected pills are now bare but for a
hairline edge in the separator colour, as unselected rows are bare. The well is the only filled pill, and it
reads at once in both appearances.

← → are the flow's whether or not something is typed, as the grid's are. In a flow they are the main way across
a line, and a filtered flow often leaves its matches on one line that ↑ ↓ cannot cross. A tree takes them only
while the field is empty, because there they open and close rather than move. A flow is never a tree
(`isTree` is false for one). One whose items hold items draws them flat, and the host warns in the plugin's log,
as it does for a refused chord.

## Observations

- Seen in a VM, in the demo's Pills and Pills Beside a Preview, in both appearances: wrapping, the cap's ellipsis
  on a long symbol name, ← → and ↑ ↓ across lines and sections, filtering to one section with its best match
  selected, and the preview beside a narrow flow.
- `debug state` reads search's rows, not a view's, so a flow's frames are seen only in captures.
- Pills draw nothing on hover, and a flow has no ×, since a pill has no room for one.
- Not tried: thousands of pills. The canvas draws only what is in sight, but the layout and widths are worked
  out for every item on each show.
