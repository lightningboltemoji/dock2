# The panel grows on springs; its rows move rather than redraw

> Change `1792647555` — 2026-10-21

## Goal

Next to the field's forms, the panel's growth and its rows felt mechanical. Results grew the panel on the
system Dock's auto-hide curve (0.35, 0.15, 0.65, 0.85) over 240 ms. It eased in, so the panel barely moved
for its first 50 ms. Every change mid-flight started a new tween from the presentation value at zero speed,
so typing quickly made the panel move, stall and move again. Forms had the same problem underneath, because
`FieldMotion`'s goal was a `Tween`. Rows were kept by index, so each keystroke rewrote row n in a single
frame, a row that moved a slot jumped there, and on a shrink the rows were cut off at once and the glass
closed over empty space.

## Implementation

**Growth.** `Growth` (Core) puts each edge of the body on a `Spring`, so a change mid-flight keeps the
velocity it has. An edge moving out has a response of 0.30 s at a damping of 0.78, about 1.7% past its place.
An edge moving in is critically damped at 0.22 s and is stopped at its place if it would cross it, so the
panel never dips into the field even when it is turned back while moving fast. These values were chosen in a
simulation against Spotlight's measured 235 ms out and 210 ms back: the edge is within 2% of its place by
175 ms out and 213 ms in. `FieldMotion` replaces its rect `Tween` with `Growth`. A form's rest waits until the
body is within 1 pt of the field, where it used to wait for the tween to end.

**One driver for every form.** The capsule used to run its own path: `CABasicAnimation`s on the glass
elements, the clip, the shadow paths and the reveal, each from its presentation value. Bringing that path to
springs would have needed either a second spring implementation or additive animations. Additive animations
cannot work, because paths (the shadow, a solid fill) cannot be added. Instead the capsule became a form with
no shapes of its own (`CapsuleSim`), so `FormDriver` plays it like any other form. The driver leaves the
capsule's field alone: no keyframes on the text or magnifier, no rest, no lens. `PanelController` loses its
capsule branches in `show`, `enter`, `hide`, `grow` and `setGlass`. `GlassView.play` now also plays the
solid-fill and border path, and places AppKit's fallback glass, so Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast
keep their motion. The rows' reveal is played from the same track: as high as the body reaches above the
field. `PanelShadow.set` keeps only Feedback's static capsule.

**Rows.** `RowsView` keeps its views by row id (made unique where a list repeats one). On `show`, a row that
stays springs to its new place with an additive `transform.translation.y` `CASpringAnimation`. It uses the
outward growth spring, so rows and glass move alike, and a second move stacks on a first without a jump. New
rows fade their content in over 0.12 s while rising 4 pt. Rows that are gone fade out in place over 0.07 s
with an ease-out, quicker than the fade-in, and then return to the spare pool. The selection band lives
outside the faded content and shows or hides at once, so a top hit whose slot doesn't change never flickers
while its row crossfades. Search's rows view is now always eight rows high. The driver's reveal clips it, so a
row that leaves fades where it was while the glass closes over it. `reach` limits hit testing to the real
rows, so the empty part above them takes no clicks. A list that was empty or not on screen shows its rows at
once, so the first results of a summon are revealed by the glass, never crossfaded from stale rows.

A row slides only if its way is clear. The first live take showed Safari, newly the top hit, sliding down
through the slot where Tailscale was fading in. Rows are text on clear glass, so the two names were drawn
over each other for about 3 frames, on the selected row. `RowsView.crosses` treats every row's way as a line
in time (a row that is arriving or leaving stays put) and finds where two of them would meet. A row that
would meet another on the way fades in at its new place instead. A block that shifts together still slides.

## Observations

- Measured at 120 fps against the live build, with Lava lamp and the capsule, driven by
  `dock2 debug type`. With the capsule, growing from 1 row to 7 (74→424 pt) took about 225 ms to get within
  2%, starting at +7 pt in the first frame. It now takes about 150 ms, starting at +31 then +71 pt, and goes
  6 pt past before settling by about 400 ms. A full growth to 8 rows goes 7.5 pt past. A forced reorder,
  where Safari becomes the top hit, now crossfades slot by slot in about 4 frames, with nothing sliding
  across another row. The background behind the panel was a live video, so the edge was traced from the
  glass's own horizontal rim rather than from a frame difference
  (scratchpad `edge.py`; the takes are not kept).
- Shrinking critically damped leaves a long tail: the last 25 pt take about 130 ms. If it feels sticky, a
  slightly underdamped inward spring that stops at its place would land sooner.
- In a crossfade, the old and new title in a slot overlap for about 2 frames. That is inherent to fading text
  in place. A faster fade-out is the knob.
- The live takes ran before the guard against stale rows from the previous summon was added. That guard is
  verified by reading the code only.
- `Dock2PluginsTests` hung in the isolated worktree used for this change. That is unrelated: the plugin module
  is untouched, and the tests probably could not find the built test guest there. `ClipboardStoreTests`
  cannot write its key from the session's sandbox. The Core suite, including four new growth tests, passes.
