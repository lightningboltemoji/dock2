# Content off the glass

> Change `1793691784` — 2026-11-02

## Goal

Views, Files and Quick Look drew their content straight on the panel's glass. The glass is frosted enough for
search's few rows, but it lets the wallpaper through behind a list, a column of files, or a detail's paragraph.
Over a photograph, faces and a red shirt showed through the file names. Content that someone reads competes
with whatever is behind it, and the panel read as a toy rather than a tool. Search's field and rows should stay
glass. The screens that hold real content should not.

## Implementation

A screen's content now sits on a **sheet** (`PanelSheet`) inside the glass. Measured on macOS 27.2, Finder's
column-view content is #282827 in dark, not `windowBackgroundColor` (#1E1E1E). An `NSVisualEffectView` of
`.contentBackground` rendered behind a window reads #282726 over the same desktop, and #FFFFFF in light, so the sheet
uses that material and follows the system, wallpaper tinting included, rather than a fixed colour. The toolbar in
the same Finder window has glass pills floating over solid content: chrome on glass, content on a sheet. That is the
rule written into `product/visual.md`.

Geometry: the sheet is inset 8 pt from the glass's sides and top, so its corners are 20.5 pt, concentric with the
panel's 28.5 pt. Its foot meets the field's glass, which keeps the field or the bar. It started at Files' 10 pt; at
8 the rim reads as a rim rather than a frame.
`PanelMetrics.sheet(over:extent:)` owns this, and the panel hands the screen the sheet's bounds, so a screen lays
out inside it with no knowledge of the glass. Files' own 10 pt inset moved to the sheet: its columns now run edge to
edge within it, and the preview takes what the narrower rim gives back (236 pt). Quick Look, in Files and from search, fills the sheet and loses
its own rounded inset, since the sheet already rounds it.

The hairline between the field and what grew stays for search's rows only, because a sheet's edge is already a
seam. The sheet fades in over `contentFadeIn` as it appears. Without that, a view pushed over a search already at the
views' height cuts from glass to sheet in one frame, since the panel does not grow and the content's reveal is
already in the past.

Toasts moved off the content. A toast sat 10 pt below the glass's top edge, which on an 8 pt rim put its top 2 pt
inside the sheet's: two edges almost touching. Rendered three ways (that, 8 pt inside the sheet, 8 pt above the
glass), the third was chosen: a toast and a confirmation now float `Feedback.gap` (the rim's 8 pt) above the glass
and ride its top edge. They live in an anchor view that `FormDriver` plays from the body's own track, as it plays the
clip and the rows' reveal, so they move in step with the glass, overshoot included. The height is clamped at the
field's top, so a resting form, smaller than the field, leaves them where the field alone would. The anchor's frame
is set where the edge comes to rest and only its transform plays the way, so a confirmation's buttons are clicked
where they stop. A dismissal now takes a toast with it, since the glass drops to the field at once as it leaves and a
toast left behind would drop with it. Above the panel, the toast's label glass showed itself clearer than the
panel's, which is the label's plus a 60% frost. It is now the panel's recipe, frosted.

Two selections assumed glass beneath them and vanished on the sheet. Files' unfocused column used black at 10%,
which is invisible on #282828; it is now `unemphasizedSelectedContentBackgroundColor`, Finder's own, measured at
#464645. The grid's cell used black at 9.5% in both appearances; it now uses the rows' band (`RowsView.band`).

Rejected for now: a solid panel with only the field as a glass capsule over it. This is Apple's window layering, but
a glass capsule over a flat fill has nothing to bend and reads as a grey pill, and the panel stops being one object
from field to Files. A sheet bled to the panel's edges with only the field's strip as glass is `sheetInset = 0`.
That is one constant away if the rim reads as a bezel.

## Observations

Captured live in dark and light, under Reduce Transparency with Increase Contrast, and recorded pushing a view from
search and opening Files from nothing. A toast was recorded at 120 fps riding the panel down from a view to search's
rows, then to the field alone, and a confirmation's Cancel was clicked where it was drawn. Not looked at: Reduce Motion, Clipboard History, Snow Leopard's style, or
`NSGlassEffectView`'s fallback. The sheet is a behind-window material inside a masked layer, and its reveal during
the panel's entrance fade was not inspected frame by frame.

- A view's rows lose 16 pt of width and 8 pt of height to the rim, so eight regular rows no longer fit their
  maximum exactly.
- The bar's Actions pill and its menu are still the label's glass, clearer than the panel's; over the field's
  frosted glass the difference is small.
- The detail's status rule now sits just above the sheet's foot, and may be redundant there.
- The field's strip under a detail or a form is empty glass apart from the bar.
