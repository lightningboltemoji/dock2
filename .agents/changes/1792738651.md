# Prompt badges and restores

> Change `1792738651` — 2026-10-22

## Goal

Badges and minimised windows felt unresponsive. A badge reached dock2 up to 1.1 s after its app set it, since
the Dock was read once a second. A minimised window's tile stayed whole for half a second after a click
restored it, then took the reshape's 0.36 s to leave, settling 0.9 s after the click, where the Dock settles in
0.47 s.

## Implementation

**Measured first**, with a test app that badges, minimises and restores itself on command, a probe that
timestamped every Accessibility notification and polled the Dock's list every 4–5 ms, and, with dock2 quit
and auto-hide turned off, a 120 fps recording of the visible Dock, its platter's edges read off one row of
pixels a frame. The recording was aligned to the probe by the press, whose gap the Dock's list frame follows.

| | Visible Dock's gap moves | First Accessibility notification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| app minimises | +45–50 ms to +500–520 ms | +552–555 ms, `AXCreated` and `AXWindowMiniaturized` together |
| app restores | +37 ms to +500 ms | +530 ms |
| `AXPress` on the item | +30 ms to +470 ms | +524 ms, `AXUIElementDestroyed` |

- **A badge has no event.** The Dock holds the new label 2–6 ms after the app sets it. Its items refuse every
  notification tried on them (`AXValueChanged`, `AXTitleChanged`, `AXLayoutChanged`, `AXStatusLabelChanged`,
  `AXAnnouncementRequested`: -25207). LaunchServices holds the label as the app's `StatusLabel`, but
  `lsappinfo listen +all` heard nothing as it changed three times in five seconds, while it heard launches and
  activations. So badges stay polled, now every 0.25 s while the dock is on screen, the clipboard watcher's
  rate: about 1 ms of reads a second. A run timer's tolerance is now a fifth of its interval rather than a
  flat 0.1 s, which would have been 40% of the new one. Ten badges through `debug state` arrived in 20–141 ms.
- **A minimise cannot be heard sooner.** Every signal comes as the genie ends, when the visible Dock's gap is
  already open; the Dock's list frame jumps then too, hidden or not. dock2 already reshapes on that
  notification, so polling faster gains nothing.
- **A restore from dock2's own tile is known at the click.** `SystemDockItems.open` leaves the item it pressed
  out of `minimised` as soon as the press succeeds, so the tile reshapes away with the genie. Filmed, the
  platter began narrowing before the genie showed and settled 360 ms on. The item stays out until the Dock lets
  it go, or until `leavingDeadline` (2 s), when one still there is shown again. A build with the deadline at
  0.1 s showed the tile go at the click, return at 104 ms and go for good at 526 ms, as the Dock let it go.

Not done: drawing a minimised window's tile as its genie starts. Neither the app nor the Dock says anything
before the genie ends, and the Dock's list frame does not move until then; private window-server
notifications were not tried.

## Observations

- A restore from an app's menu in dock2 (`AppWindows.raise`) still waits for the Dock, as does one the app
  makes itself: only a press says the item is leaving.
- A minimised window's tile still arrives ~0.55 s after the minimise starts, then reshapes in over 0.36 s; the
  visible Dock is settled at ~0.5 s. Shortening the arrival is a visual call, not taken here.
- With the app frozen (`kill -STOP`) the Dock still restored the window and let the item go, so the deadline
  was exercised only by shortening it.
- The Dock's own item and the app's notification reach `DockController` on separate hops, so a minimised tile
  can be laid out once unmatched and again matched a frame later.
