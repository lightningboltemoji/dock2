# Files' pills say where the tab is, as wide as that takes

> Change `1794037021` — 2026-11-06

## Goal

Two things were wrong with the pills. A pill read one folder behind the path: with `~/a/b` selected, the path ended on
`b` and the pill said `a`. And every pill was at least 84 pt, so a folder called `1` sat at the front of a capsule
twice as long as it needed.

## Implementation

**The name.** Selecting a folder opens its column and leaves the keys where they were, so `Tab.folder`, the column with
the keys, is one behind the selection whenever a folder is selected. That is the right answer for the path bar and a
paste, and the wrong one for a pill. `Tab.deepest` is the last column open.

A pill's title alone would have been worse than the bug: it would say `b` and take a drop into `a`. So everything that
treats a tab as a destination moved together: name, plate, tooltip, drop, ring, and ⌘K's Copy to and Move to. The ring's
guard for the tab showing moved too, or the tab showing would have ringed for a copy into its own deepest folder.

**The width.** The 84 pt floor stops being a size a pill starts from and becomes how far one gives way. That alone would
have wasted room: an even share gives a short pill more than it uses, and six tabs with three short names would have left
the long three at 84 pt with about 80 pt unused. `PanelMetrics.fit` gives what the narrow leave to the wide, so they get about
110 pt. It sits in Core so it can be tested: Shell has no test target.

Rejected: a smaller floor, which only moves the problem to a shorter name; and centring the label in the pill, which keeps
the empty capsule.

Driven in a VM on macOS 27.2, before and after. Before: the path at `alpha > beta` with the pill on `alpha`, and a pill
84 pt wide for a four-letter name. After, with both tabs on real folders: the pill follows selecting a folder, entering it,
selecting a file (back to the folder holding it) and ← (stays on the folder left open); `1`, `2`, `3` beside long names
at 47 pt or so; six tabs sharing the room; a drag onto a pill landing in `~/tabs/1/2` and not `~/tabs/1`; ⌘K naming the
same folder as the pill, and copying 8,066 items there; a ring filling on a pill reading `q` during a copy of 2.6 GB to
another volume, the file in `q` and not `p`.

## Observations

- A copy on one volume of those 8,066 items showed no ring in captures 0.45 s apart. It was not timed, so that it ended
  inside the 0.5 s before a ring shows is likely rather than measured. The cross-volume copy is what showed the ring.
- An operation into a tab's `folder` while a folder is selected there no longer rings that tab's pill, since the pill
  names somewhere else. The banner still shows it.
- On a pill this short the close button covers more of it: about 28 pt of 47 pt on hover, leaving about 19 pt that shows
  the tab. A click on the name and one on the × both did the right thing, driven through `debug click`. Whether a person
  hits the × meaning to switch is untested.
- With six tabs the strip keeps a trailing gap where New Tab was, as it did.
- The tooltip was not read back: it takes the same `folder` as the drop, which was.
- Network shares and iCloud folders were not driven; `deepest` is a column's URL like any other.
- Only macOS 27.2 was run. `tahoe-base` was stopped.
- `aWatchHearsChangesUntilItsFolderGoes`, in the plugin tests, failed once in a full `make test` and passed five times
  alone and in the next full run. Nothing here touches it, and whether it flakes without this change was not checked.
