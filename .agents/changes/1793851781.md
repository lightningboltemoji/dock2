# The pane is the system's control glass

> Change `1793851781` — 2026-11-04

## Goal

The pane had no depth. In light it was a white card in a grey frame, nothing like the sidebar search field in System
Settings, which reads as a slab in both appearances. The tuning behind it was not to be trusted.

## Implementation

Measured through the real field, top to bottom, in light: ground 238, a dark line (217, and 173 at the ends), a white
lip of 255 two pixels thick, a fall over 6–10 pt to a face of 234, *darker* than the ground, and a faint shadow below.
Through the pane: ground 236, then 255 and flat. The pane's face was clipped at white, so its 0.25 rim was white on
white. It had no falloff, and its backdrop drew nothing outside its shape.

So nothing here is fitted to that profile. A probe hosting SwiftUI's `.searchable` sidebar field matched System Settings
within a level at every sampled pixel, and walking its layers gave the system's recipe
([observing](../OBSERVING.md#reading-the-systems-own-glass)). The same inputs in `GlassLayer`'s tree came within a few
levels of it. `IMPLEMENTATION.md` §3 lists what gives it its body and where the pane departs from it.

The recipe overturns three decisions. `1793778703` turned the rim's diffuse halo off because it isn't the same top and
bottom; that falloff is most of what reads as thickness. It also kept `curvature` at 1 or above because lower saturates
a straight edge; the system saturates it on purpose, at 0.75. And it made every edge the same top and bottom, where
the system's light is lit from above. The pane's soft glow from within, the only use of `Glow`, is gone with them.

The face no longer lifts in light: the system caps it, at 239 here. The ground went from a 7.5% to a 13% shade (222) so a
selection still has somewhere to go, as far below the face as the dark ground is, 17 levels. Copying the field's 233 on a 238 ground was tried first: it matches the field, but
the selection has nothing to cut down to. In dark the field's face of 89 is too bright over a whole panel, so black
0.07 gives 57.

**The selection** is rebuilt on what a cut through a slab looks like. A black `KeyFillHighlight` line was tried and stays
near black at any amount: 34 of 255 at curvature 0.75, 53 at 1, 114 at 3, with `fillAmount` 0 turning the hole black. So
`Glow` came back with a `drop`, for a smooth ramp down from the top wall. The foot's light moved inside the band: at
`outset` 1 it never drew, for a reason not pinned down. It stays plain, since through vibrancy it is 227 against 243.

**One height.** Tuned alone, that shadow cut far deeper than the pane stood: on the same ground, in light, the pane cast 3
levels and the top wall 28, and in dark 0 and 13, with the hole shaded on every side, foot included. The two are one edge
over one ground, so they are now one shadow, `paneCast`: outward beneath the pane, inward in the hole, and they read
the same pixel for pixel. Sweeping the filter first separated its inputs: `inputRingShadowOpacity` darkens only inside the
face, and `inputShadowOpacity` alone casts on the ground, but the margin below ends it abruptly about 8 pt past the foot,
a hard step once it is strong enough to see. So the filter's shadow is off and `Glow` is `Cast`. It sits beneath the
backdrop, since over it the foot's lip fell from 255 to 240. Inward it starts a point in from its shape, leaving a lighter
strip around the hole, so it is grown by one, and the edge moved inside `GroundInset`'s well to clip that. Its strength
is a judgement, compared by eye at 8.5% and 14% in light: 16 levels past the edge, against 3 and 28 before.

**The flip.** A first port had the shadow falling up: the ground was brighter below the field than above it. AppKit's
tree is flipped. `isGeometryFlipped` fixes it, but moves every shape and would reach the platter, so the offset is
negated in the recipe.

**The leak.** The port drew the pane's top edge weaker than its foot in the app, 225/238 against 255/255, and not on the
bench. The difference was the desktop. Over black and then white, the dark top lip was 93 and 180, with a band below it
moving 30 levels. The foot sits on the 56 pt field strip, but the top and sides have 6 pt of ground, and the system's 16 pt
margin and 8 pt blur fill reach past it. The margin is now `paneInset`, and the fill is off. In the app, over black and
white, the light top edge is identical and the dark one within 2 levels.

Rejected: a vibrant tint over the face, the system's way of darkening it. On a solid ground it is one more layer to
get one more number, and `MaxLumaSDR` and black do the same. `blendsThrough()` on the tree, which the probe's tree has:
removing it changed no pixel. `inputBleed*`, `inputSDR*` and the output effect's `maximum`: removing them changed no pixel,
so only `inputBleedOpacity` 0 stays.

Measured in both appearances: search, Files with its focused selection, a list, a detail, a toast, Reduce Transparency in
both, and Increase Contrast in light; a recording of the panel growing, shrinking and growing again, read as contact
sheets. The list, the detail, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast were not repeated after the cast.

## Observations

- The rim's amount is 0.7 against the system's 0.5, and still a few levels short of AppKit's at the lip. Why this tree
  draws it dimmer is unknown; every property read off the two trees matches. It does not change the look.
- Screenshots clip at 255, so what `inputMaxHeadroom` 1.2, `inputClamp` over 1 and the rim's clamp of 1.2 do on an EDR
  display is unmeasured. They are the system's values; the lip may be brighter on the display than any capture shows.
- Past the top and foot, a few pixels at the pane's sides still answer to the backdrop: 55 and 64 in dark, 228 and 233
  in light, on the two pixels inside the side line. The top lip's are within 2.
- The pane's corners were checked by eye and only over one desktop, since a margin may reach past the ground's
  rounded corner where the rim is widest diagonally.
- The field at rest, the label, the pill and the platter keep their fitted recipes. The field has none of the rim's
  halo, and may want it; the platter is fitted to the real Dock and should be measured before anything moves it.
- Increase Contrast was captured in light only, and only search's selection was profiled.
- A toast's pane has 6 pt of ground below its foot and the cast reaches about 7 pt. Nothing shows, but it was checked by eye.
- One `make test` failed while this was being built, and its output was lost. It did not recur: the suite passed on
  the change both with dock2 running and without, and on clean `main`.
