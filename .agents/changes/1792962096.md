# The gem comes and goes as one

> Change `1792962096` — 2026-10-25

## Goal

Three faults with the gem, as reported, each measured on the release build at 60 fps over the real desktop:

- **Dismissed, the gem outlived its glass.** `hide` faded the glass view's alpha, the field and the shadow. The faces
  are a view of their own beneath the glass, and nothing faded them. The last two frames of every dismissal showed the
  bare, unbent gem.
- **Every open flashed.** Scored against the first frame of the take, the region around the field jumped for two
  frames to one fixed state on each of four entrances: the marble solid and opaque, at its final place and size, with
  no gem, no shadow and no fade. It then dropped to nearly nothing and the real fade-in began. That state is what
  `GlassLayer.rebuild()` leaves: elements placed at the track's last sample and nothing else. A `CATransaction` with
  none around it commits the moment it ends. `GlassView.play` and `rebuild` are such transactions, so the bare tree
  reached the window server while the main thread went on building the faces' keyframes and the panel's fade mask.
  Those landed with the turn's implicit commit about two frames later. Timed with probes, the entrance spent 16 ms
  baking and 15 ms building layers on release, and 250 ms and 23 ms on the debug build. Most of the bake was the 24 s
  rest loop, simulated again at 240 Hz for every showing because each showing gets a new driver.
- **Clearing was in your face.** At the clear, all twenty faces came up at full strength in their slots across the
  field, sharp because the lens came back at once. The glass then hugged them on an underdamped spring while they
  folded in. The pour does the opposite: the lens lets go, the faces blur, and they fade as they fly.
- **Found on the way.** The resting marble was 92 × 76, not round. Hugging the faces, the glass took its extent from
  the marble's own edge before subtracting its 8 pt margin, so it was always 16 pt wider than 76.

## Implementation

- **One commit per change.** `Motion.together` wraps a change in one transaction, so the nested ones commit with it.
  `FormDriver.change` runs inside it, as do the panel's entrance, its dismissal and an appearance change, which builds
  the glass again and replays the motion on it.
- **The rest loop is worked out once** for each form and field, in a static cache, instead of once per showing.
- **Dismissal fades the panel as one.** Everything the panel shows except `Feedback` lives in a container (`stack`).
  The entrance's radial mask and the dismissal's plain mask both go on it, so the gem stays under its glass until both
  are gone. A notice after a dismissal stays visible; that was checked with a config that cannot be read.
- **The lens is part of the track.** `FormFrame.lens` runs from 0 to 1. `GlassLayer.playLens` keyframes each lens input
  on the backdrop, from the recipe's own value toward the lens's, beside the shapes, replacing `setLensed`'s
  animation at the event. The pour lets go of the lens on a critically damped spring of 0.25 s, in place of the 0.24 s
  slide curve.
- **The gem comes back as it broke apart, the other way.** The glass draws in to the marble critically damped at the
  pour's response, height included, as the lava lamp's does. Each face's distance home follows the square of how far
  the glass is still open, on a 0.06 s spring, so a visible face is inside the glass. A face shows again once it is
  past 55% of the way home, the mirror of the pour fading it past 55% out. The lens returns once every face has. The
  marble is round, 76 across. The magnifier shows only where the glass covers its place, so it is gone as the end
  passes it rather than lingering just outside.
- **Tests.** `theGemComesBackAsItBrokeApart` runs from 18 moments of the rest. It checks that the glass never grows back
  or passes the marble, that a visible face lies inside the body and within 160 pt of the middle, that the lens only
  rises, and that the magnifier is never outside the glass. Against the old return there were 10,429 ticks with a
  visible face past 160 pt and 1,971 ticks of the glass growing back, and the marble never got narrower than 90 pt.
  With the magnifier's coverage off, its check fails 577 times.
- **Rejected.** Fading the faces with the glass's alpha: through half-clear glass the unbent faces show directly, a
  double image. Faces on their own springs into a glass drawing in on its own: the glass's edge cuts them where it
  masks them. Hugging the faces only while they are visible: the glass would jump as each one appeared.

## Observations

- After, on release, filming open, wait 1 s, close, wait 1 s four times: each entrance climbs from its first frame with
  no fixed frames ahead of it, and each dismissal fades marble and gem together. The lava lamp and the drop climb in the
  same way.
- The entrance's bake no longer simulates the loop, which was about 15 of its 31 ms on release; this was not timed
  again. Every change at rest still converts the 480-sample loop into layer values, about 14 ms on release.
- A track still begins at the media time it was baked, so a change shows from as far into it as the main thread took
  to commit it. On release that is a few frames of the entrance's rise, under its fade. On the debug build it is the
  whole rise.
- The gem's entrance still kicks the marble's height (`arrive`) on an underdamped spring.
- Three takes over a flat backdrop window showed no flash that the same loop showed every time over the desktop.
  `OBSERVING.md` now says to film a reported glitch over the real desktop as well.
