# A form that fits

> Change `1793692713` — 2026-11-02

## Goal

The demo's Every Field form drew badly: its title clipped at the top, the date picker over the Plan pop-up, the
folder field an empty sliver beside Choose…, and the rest cut off. The fault was in dock2's renderer, not the demo,
so any plugin's form of more than a few fields drew the same way.

## Implementation

`FormComponent` pinned its `NSStackView` to the view's bounds, with no scroll view. Once the fields were taller than
the view, Auto Layout could satisfy the stack only by breaking the controls' compression resistance, and it squeezed
whatever gave first. The stack now lives in a flipped document inside an `NSScrollView`, pinned to the clip view's
top and width and as tall as its fields, so nothing is ever squeezed.

Each field is a group of its label, its control and its error, 6 pt apart, with 18 pt between groups. A label now
reads with its own control rather than floating between two. Text, secure text, multi-line text and folders take
the column's width, from the sheet's edge less 24 pt, where a detail's text starts. That replaces a fixed 560 pt,
which was wider than the column on the sheet. A choice, a date and a toggle stay as wide as they read. An error
wraps instead of running off.

`FolderField` laid out its row by autoresizing from a zero frame and pinned its own height to 24 pt. The row's text
field has no intrinsic width, so it collapsed. The row is now pinned by constraints, and the text hugs loosely so it
takes what Choose… leaves.

Tab past the fold: a text field keeps its field editor inside its own clip view, so AppKit's scroll-to-visible stops
there and never reaches the form's scroll view. The form observes the window's `firstResponder` and scrolls the
group holding it into view. For the first group it scrolls to the top, so the title shows when Tab wraps around.

## Observations

Seen live: Every Field at rest and with three errors, More Fields Than Fit tabbed down past the fold and wrapped
back, and Every Field stepped through its date picker to the folder field.

- A choice and a toggle are not Tab stops unless Keyboard Navigation is on in System Settings: AppKit's rule, not
  changed here.
- Nothing shows that a form continues below the fold except the overlay scroller while it moves. A fade at the
  sheet's foot would say so for lists too.
- A multi-line field still ends its editing on ↩ rather than taking a new line.
