# Files rides the glass in and out

> Change `1794031560` — 2026-11-06

## Goal

Opening Files felt slow, and closing it felt abrupt. Filmed at 60 fps in a VM, default form, from the first visible
frame: the glass stood empty for about 300 ms, and the contents were solid only about 450 ms in. Warm and cold were
alike, so this was the motion and not loading. `1792895169` made contents wait for the glass, so that it would never cut
them, and noted the cost. Closing, `closeAll` took the contents off a frame before the panel moved, leaving an empty box
to collapse.

## Implementation

**What rides what.** Nothing waits for the glass now. `ScreenRide` (Core, pure) says where each part stands while the
glass is at a given body, and `FormDriver` plays it from the same sampled track as the glass, so it holds through every
form and any change mid-flight. `FormFrame.holds`, `holdTolerance` and the driver's reveal are gone.

`1792895169` rejected carrying the bar's ends because the columns would "still be cut by the clip", and riding the
columns too because it "would slide a 940 pt layout sideways". The second was never filmed. With the contents pinned to
the glass's top left corner the layout does not read as sliding: it stays still against the glass, like a window resized
from that corner. The column's rows are the part that matters, and they are never cut. The glass still cuts the right
side, for about 0.1 s while the sides arrive. That is the cost of this design.

**The bar.** `PathBar`'s path moved into `leadingEnd`, so that it and the tabs can ride apart. The ends start closer
together than they rest, and a long path ran under the `+` for three frames at 60 fps, so the leading end has a mask
that ends at the tabs and rides the gap between the ends (`ScreenRide.room`), its fade in the 16 pt the layout already
leaves. Every control was driven again after the move: tabs, `+`, the places menu, a typed path, ⌘F.

**Leaving.** A screen going back to nothing or to the results stays in its slot, riding the glass and fading out, then
goes (`leave`, `endLeaving`). It takes no clicks meanwhile (`PanelView.passesClicks`), since its screen is closed. The
results fade in under it, or their rows stood over Files' for three frames. `hide` shrinks a grown panel with a screen
on it rather than snapping it to the capsule, which would have jumped the contents; search's own dismissal still snaps.
A leaving panel stays solid (`FieldMotion.leave(solid:)`) rather than going clear with contents still on it.

**From nothing.** `enter()` started the form and `showScreen` poured it within one turn, so a form nobody saw cost a
pour of about 0.4 s on the glass's sides. `showScreen` now starts it over as the field when the panel has not arrived.
The dock-away path always did. The field's "Search" no longer shows for a moment under the bar.

**Alternatives, filmed side by side.** Bar first, columns waiting: the columns still land at 200–300 ms. Uncovering the
columns in place, as search's rows are: the rows are top-aligned, so the glass reaches last what is read first, the top
row at about 150 ms against about 50 ms riding. Zoom, the contents scaled with the glass: very close, and it keeps the
right column whole, but the type stands at about 70% beside a bar at full size and the rows change pitch as they land.
Kept riding for true-size type; zoom is about ten lines in `FormDriver.ride`, if it is ever preferred. It would need
clamping for a form poured from rest, where the glass starts far narrower than the field.

Measured in a VM, default form, deep folder with the preview, from the first visible frame: bar there at once, against
about 300 ms; rows readable about 50–100 ms in, against about 350 ms; everything in place by about 150–220 ms, against
about 450 ms. Stress: 25 open/close back to back, 10 raced, 15 push/pop inside the fade, then contents solid and a row
click landing, same process. Also filmed: capsule, dock away, Files over a query, Esc back, a demo view, Reduce Motion,
dark. `kill -9` still gives the Dock back.

A push landing inside the leave is safe: 15 back to back in 6 rounds stayed up on both builds, each Esc polled until it had
taken effect. Sent without the poll, the new build was dismissed in all 6 rounds and the old in none, because a `dock2 files`
overtook the posted Esc (`OBSERVING.md`). Every step after that was the design's, and a real key cannot be overtaken this
way. Why the new build loses that race more is not known; it is not the main thread, which answers `status` in the same 133 ms
just after an open on both builds.

## Observations

- A folder never listed before holds up the first frame: 8,000 files took 700–800 ms in a VM on both builds, 75 ms once
  cached. `showFiles` calls `go(to:)` before pushing, and `DirectoryListing.list` runs on the main thread. No animation
  can help. The fix is to open first and list off the main thread. It is the largest latency left in opening Files.
- Under Reduce Motion, a close shows the rows at full strength over clear glass with the capsule behind them. Same on
  the old build: it is how `Motion.crossfade` snapshots the panel.
- Going back from Files, the magnifier and text sit too close for about 50 ms ("Qsaf"). Same on the old build.
- Under a bar, a query's text fades out beside the place for about 50 ms. It reads as a crossfade.
- Quick Look's preview loads after the contents show, so its image changes once. The wait hid that.
- A view replaced by a node of another kind mid-growth stops riding until the next change. Not seen; the window is
  short.
- Clicks during a leave were reasoned about, not driven: 0.1 s is too short for `debug click`.
- Only macOS 27.2 was filmed. `tahoe-base` was stopped, so Tahoe is unseen, as is a real GPU. Timings are a VM's: about
  55 ms of each first frame there is the command line.
