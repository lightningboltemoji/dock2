# Files arrives frosted

> Change `1792740352` — 2026-10-23

## Goal

Opening Files from nothing, from the Finder tile or `dock2 files`, flashed in dark mode: the panel first
showed light and then darkened to its settled tint over about a fifth of a second. Clipboard History and a
plugin's view opened from nothing did the same. Recorded at 120 fps over a dark terminal with the
installed build, the mean of the panel's area went from 45.7 (the background) up to 57 in one frame and then
down to 42.4 over 0.24 s, on every opening.

## Implementation

`grow` always faded the frost in over the slide, from however frosted the glass was. `show()` resets the
glass to clear, and every screen opened from nothing (`showFiles`, `showClipboardHistory`,
`showPluginView`, and the reveal's handover, which calls `showFiles`) requests the keyboard and pushes its
screen in one call. So the panel's first frame was the clear field's glass at full strength, since growth also
ends the entrance's fade. In dark mode that glass is the label's lifted face (white 0.91, black 0.17), far
lighter than the frost (11% grey at 0.6) it then faded to.

The frost now animates only once the panel has been on screen: `hasArrived` is cleared by `show()` and
set at the end of the turn in which the entrance began (`arrive()`, from `enter()` and from the in-place
path under Reduce Motion). Growth before then, in the same turn or while the entrance waits for a dock
still to arrive, sets the frost at once. A letter typed into a field already showing still frosts over the
slide, as designed. A letter typed in the 0.1 s before a hidden dock arrives now lands frosted at once, since
nothing of the field had been drawn yet.

The rejected alternative was to have `showFiles` and the others push the screen before taking the keyboard,
with `show()` entering already grown. That changes the order focus and the views' stack are set up in, across
three call sites, to fix what is one rule about the frost.

## Observations

Measured live with the debug build in dark mode, opening and dismissing in a loop: with the dock hidden
(the entrance waiting), with it held up (`debug reveal on`, the entrance starting in the same call), for
Clipboard History, and under `DOCK2_REDUCE_MOTION=1`. Each opening now darkens monotonically from the
background to 42.5, with no frame above it. Light mode was not recorded. There, too, the frost is set at
once rather than faded in, since the change does not depend on appearance.

The panel still appears whole in its first frame rather than fading in, because growth ends the entrance's
fade ("what grows shows whole"). Over a light background it now arrives at its frosted tint, but it still
arrives at once.
