# Files' bar is a row

> Change `1793864209` — 2026-11-04

## Goal

Functionally Files was right and it still felt unnatural, with the Home selector the worst of it. The Home selector was one
control doing two jobs: a crumb that could not take you Home, and a pop-up whose only sign was ⌃⌄ on bare ground. Behind that
was a wider problem. Files was a Finder path bar and column view dropped into a panel with a language of its own: the glyph and
text lines, plates, four sizes of type. Its 15 pt was none of the four, and its 22 pt rows were off the grid. On a Mac tinted
Graphite, as this one is, the system's folders and selection are grey too, so nothing in Files had a colour at all.

## Implementation

**The bar is a row.** Rather than restyle the selector, the bar takes a search result's shape: the place on a plate on the
glyph line, the path at 16 pt from the text line. The plate is the magnifier's size, not a result's 32 pt, which was too loud at
rest in a bar: it stands where the magnifier does, at the magnifier's weight. That splits the two jobs. The plate is the control and every crumb is a
crumb, so `Home` now goes Home, and ⌃⌄ and `PillButton.accessory` with it are gone. A path typed or ⌘F's query starts on the same
line, and their glyph sits where the plate does, so a change of mode no longer moves anything. The plate shows the place
*holding* the folder rather than the root, so Home › Downloads › x is green. `Segment.symbol` went with the crumb's icon.

**Colour says where.** Each place has a plate colour, the rule `visual.md` already set for what dock2 does: on a Graphite Mac it
is the only colour in Files, and it means something. It is on the bar, the menu and each tab, whose pills were rows of identical
grey folders. Rejected: a capsule around the old selector, which keeps the fused control and only dresses it.

**The menu rises.** The places' menu rose over the crumb as a pop-up button's does, half covering the plate and running off
towards the dock. `visual.md` already says the bar's menu floats over the pane, so both bar menus now rise from it, as a tile's
does. `PlaceMenuRow` gained the plate, and 28 pt rows to match, or a disk's row would have sat out of step in the same menu.

**Menus keep up.** Every dock2 menu lagged, with no panel up too: a highlight trailed the pointer by up to 750 ms, where a plain
app's holds under 30. dock2 is never active, so it is sent only some of the pointer's moves, and a system menu tracks from those.
Hover already reads the monitors for this reason, and menus now share them: while one is up, `PointerMonitor` posts each move
it hears into dock2's own queue. Making dock2 active for the menu was rejected: it dims the app in front and sets off the
dismissal that hangs on activation. The plates, the panel's glass, the lava lamp and the windows behind were each measured
and ruled out first.

**The rows.** 28 pt rather than 22, settling `visual.md`'s open question on the grid. 24 was the other candidate, and is
one constant away. The band goes to radius 10, near concentric with what it holds at that height. The › drops to 9 pt in the
tertiary colour, because twenty of them per column were the busiest thing in the pane.

**The preview.** A thumbnail was a sharp rectangle with no edge against the pane, so it now fits a frame of its own, rounded and
shadowed, a page lying on the glass, and fades in over the icon instead of popping. An icon keeps its own shape and shadow. The
type moves to the panel's sizes, which pushed `Modified` past its column, so a date is this year's to the minute and an older
one's to the day, as the sketch in `product/files` always had it.

**Motion** is deliberately small. A place lands on a spring, the plate lifts under the pointer, and a folder's column slides out
14 pt from the one that opened it. Only a single new column arrives, because a tab or a path arriving column by column reads as
noise. Reduce Motion takes the movement and keeps the fades.

## Observations

Checked on the live panel in light and dark: the plates, the menu with an ejecting server, the preview, ⌘F and a typed path,
hover. In light: tabs in three places, and `Home` and `Developer` clicked and Downloads chosen from the menu, each landing
there. The arrival was filmed and read at 40 fps. Run under `DOCK2_REDUCE_MOTION`. A server's ⏏ was drawn but not pressed. A menu's
worst lag, gliding over a flat backdrop: the places' 753 to 52 ms and a tile's 476 to 33, both on the debug build, and a column's
right-click menu measured after.

- The first build of the arrival crashed: `columns[arriving...]` traps when a new tab has fewer columns than the last. The guard
  gave everything back. Fixed by the single-column rule, and the same path was run again through tabs.
- 28 pt rows trade density for air: 15.5 rows at rest where `1793863471` left 19.7.
- Drops on the plate do nothing. The first crumb still takes them.
- The plate opens on a click alone, with no key, as the crumb did.
- Files at Home with nothing selected is one column and three empty slots. It is the largest thing left, and a decision about
  what Files opens to rather than about its look, so it was left.
- A selection's glide between rows, as a spring rather than a jump, would suit the rest. Not tried: the band belongs to each row
  in an `NSTableView`.
- Colours are by eye, on the system's own tints. The plate for a local disk and a folder outside any place is gray on purpose.
