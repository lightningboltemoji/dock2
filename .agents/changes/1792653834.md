# The tiles fade in from half

> Change `1792653834` — 2026-10-22

## Goal

The entrance faded each tile, its dot and the separators in from nothing, so the wave read as items
appearing rather than popping onto a platter they were already on. The fade's duration was tunable; how
far it fell was not.

## Implementation

`EntryMotion.popOpacity` is the share of a layer's own opacity the fade starts from, beside `popScale`, the
share of its size the spring starts from. `DockView.fadeIn` multiplies it by the layer's model opacity rather
than starting at a fixed value, so a dimmed tile still lands at its dimness and the dot of an app not running
stays at none throughout. Tiles, dots and separators share the one constant, since they enter on the same
wave.

The entrance was retuned with it, by eye: `popOpacity` 0.5, `popScale` 0.75 (from 0.85), `popFade` 0.5 s
(from 0.15 s).

## Observations

Not recorded or measured; the values are a starting point for tuning by eye. The fade now outlasts the
spring's rise, so a tile reaches its peak before it is fully opaque.
