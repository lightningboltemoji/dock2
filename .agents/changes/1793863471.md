# Files clears the pane's corners

> Change `1793863471` — 2026-11-04

## Goal

Files' content ran into the panel's corners. It kept Finder's insets, measured off a window with a far tighter corner, on
a pane whose corners `1793861542` had just made 28 pt, and that change left Files' bands alone. A selected top row stood
10.5 pt from the side and 6 pt from the top, which sounds roomy and leaves its corner 2.3 pt from the pane's turn, where
search's bands keep 8. Scrolled rows were cut by the curve; ⌘F's last row and Quick Look's text were too, with the `e` of
`enum` sliced off at the bottom left. Four 236 pt columns left 4 pt of the pane, so the fourth divider ran down inside the
corner. The bar's icon stood 22 pt in and the rows' 23, close enough to look like a mistake.

## Implementation

One rule, `PanelMetrics.cornerInset`: what is too small to share the pane's corners stands 12 pt in on every side. It is
not picked by eye. A 22 pt band cannot be concentric with 28 pt corners, so the question is how far in its 6 pt corner
must stand to keep a band's 8 pt from the turn, and that answer is 12: 7.9 by the geometry, and about 8 read off a capture
along the corner's diagonal. `whatCannotShareThePanesCornersClearsThem` holds it.

Everything else lands on the grid as a result. With the band at 12, an icon 8 pt in from it stands 20 pt from the pane,
which is the panel's 26 pt `margin`, so Files' rows join the line search's icons stand on, the chevron ends on the margin
opposite, and the preview's text takes the same inset. The bar's first crumb moves so its icon sits exactly over theirs,
and New Tab's plus ends on the margin opposite. Moving the rows alone would have grown the 1 pt mismatch with the bar to 4.

`columnWidth` is now a quarter of the pane, 237 pt, so three columns and the preview, or four columns, fill it exactly, and
a column the pane's edge ends drops its divider. Keeping 236 and only hiding that line would have left 4 pt of unaccounted
space on the right.

Scrolled rows fade between 4 and 12 pt from the pane's top and foot, a mask on each column's scroll view, and the scroller
stops on the margin. The fade ends where a row rests, so nothing at rest is touched. Search's way, a hard clip 8 pt in, was
rejected here: it suits 56 pt rows, and on a 22 pt row beside edge-to-edge dividers it reads as an invisible line cutting
text. Insetting only the first and last columns would have put the rows of neighbouring columns out of step.

Quick Look is a `QuickLookCard` for Files and ⌘Y alike, the same `QLPreviewView` in the same defect, so one view rather than
two patches: 12 pt in, 16 pt corners, concentric. Its own text starts about 8 pt in, so at 8 pt in and 20 pt corners it would
still come within about 2 pt of the card's turn, and the card needs a card's inset. Being inset meant the columns would show
round it, and they already showed through a folder's transparent preview, so they go. They go by `alphaValue`: `isHidden` took
first responder from the column, the space that closes Quick Look stopped arriving, and ⌘F then opened behind it. A capture
of the same run caught that. The banner takes the same inset and radius, which were 10 and 12, and it was 7.5 pt from the turn.

over a running job in the banner, three tabs, and a posted scroll down the last column. Files opening was recorded, and the
rows, bands and dividers come in with the glass with no pop.
