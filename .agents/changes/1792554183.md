# An entrance: the tiles pop, the field rises

> Change `1792554183` — 2026-10-20

## Goal

dock2 came on screen flatly. The tiles rode the platter's slide as one, and the field grew out of a sliver
at the platter's top. The ask was an entrance with some life: the tiles appearing like Fiber's pins, with
a light bounce, and the search field rising as if from the bottom of the screen, zooming in a little, and
fading in with a wave like Fiber's tab overlay.

## Implementation

**The tiles.** Slid in from all the way away, `DockView.popIn` gives each icon a `CASpringAnimation` on
`transform.scale` from 0.75 to 1 (perceptual 0.45 s, bounce 0.45), and a 0.15 s fade with its dot. The
separators fade as the wave passes. The wave is Fiber's pin wave (`EntryMotion.waveDelays`, unit-tested):
0.12 s to the farthest tile and 12 ms more a tile, from the pointer's x when the pointer brought the dock
up, else from the platter's middle. `DockController` pops only after a slide out has finished
(`isAway`, set in the completion that clears the glass). A dock called back mid-hide just slides back. The
tiles still ride the slide, so they stay on the glass.

Recordings at 120 fps, with the centre icon's green measured frame by frame, set the timing and the spring:

- Starting the pop with the slide hid it: the icon cleared the screen's edge at 97% of its size. The wave
  now starts halfway through the slide (`popLead`).
- Bounce 0.3 from 0.6 overshot 2%, which was invisible. 0.4 from 0.5 overshot 5% (101→106 pt), and the
  user found that the right effect but too big a change in size. The side tiles started too small to read
  as apps. So the pop now runs 0.75→1 at bounce 0.45, and measured +3% (104 pt at its peak).

**The field.** `PanelController.enter` moves the glass's SDF shape from `EntryMotion.riseStart`, 32 pt lower
at 94% about the centre, to its place over 0.36 s on (0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1), Fiber's overlay curve. The shadow
goes the same way. The field's contents go the same way through a `transform` animation alone, computed
about the layer's anchor since AppKit owns the layer. The fade is a radial `CAGradientLayer` mask on the
panel's view, so one mask covers glass, shadow and contents. Its opacity runs 0→1 over 0.15 s, while its
locations carry a front from the field's bottom centre at 1,800 pt/s: whole behind a 160 pt soft edge, half
past it. That is Fiber's recipe: its panel wave dims to 0.75 past the front over a quick overall fade.

The first try was a full reveal: the mask was transparent past the front. It read as a grey smear, since a
640 × 57 capsule kept its ends faded for most of the motion. A mask on an ancestor of a `CABackdropLayer`
was the risk, and recordings showed the glass rendering through it. Dismissal plays the rise back in 0.18 s
on (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1), the glass fading as it sinks. It has no wave, because the notice after a dismissal
lives in the same view and a mask would hide it.

**Order.** The summon emits `takeKeyboard` before `holdDock`, so the field's rise had finished before the
dock arrived. Rather than reorder `Interaction`'s effects, which would change their contract and its tests,
`takeKeyboard` passes `EntryMotion.fieldLag` (0.1 s) when the dock is not shown. The field has the keyboard
throughout, so typing ahead can grow the panel during the lag. `grow` now runs a waiting entrance at once
and takes the mask off. Without that, glass grew in full and the late entrance collapsed it back to the
field. `entrances` counts every show, so a stale delayed entrance never fires.

**Reduce Motion's appearance was broken.** The field and its glass appeared in one frame (recorded
between 360 and 375 ms after ⌘space). The old branch set `alphaValue = 0` and animated it to 1 in the same
transaction, which animates from the value it replaced. The panel now fades in place through the same mask.
`show` also removes what a dismissal still has running on the field's and glass's own layers.

`PanelMetrics.seed` is gone.

Verified by recording the bottom of the screen at 120 fps with ScreenCaptureKit, driving ⌘space, Esc and
the pointer from the recorder: summon with the dock hidden, a reveal at the dock's left end (wave from
Finder), a parked pointer at its right (wave from Ghostty), summon/dismiss/summon at 80 and 20 ms gaps with
the dock hiding and with it held up, typing "gh" 20 ms after ⌘space, and runs under
`DOCK2_REDUCE_MOTION` and `DOCK2_REDUCE_TRANSPARENCY`. `make test` passes.

## Observations

- With auto-hide off the dock never comes in, so the tiles never pop. Only the field rises. Popping the
  tiles on summon there, or at launch, is a choice still to make.
- Every number is tuned by eye on one Mac at tile size 128 with three tiles. A long dock's wave and smaller
  tiles' +3% were not seen.
- Typing ahead in the lag shows the result rows at their place while the field is still rising beneath
  them, for about 0.1 s.
- Dark mode was not recorded. The mask is alpha alone and the recipe is unchanged.
- The dismissal's glass fade is an AppKit alpha animation. A re-summon removes it rather than replacing
  it, and that was seen at a 20 ms gap with the dock held up.
