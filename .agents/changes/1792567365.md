# Clipboard History's balance, and deleting from it

> Change `1792567365` — 2026-10-21

## Goal

Clipboard History had the right shape and the wrong weights. The list beside the preview used search's
Spotlight rows — 56 pt, a 16.5 pt title — in 45% of the panel, so titles truncated early and outweighed the
preview. Where an entry came from, when, and its size were metadata rows at the end of the text, about eight
lines that scrolled away with it. Deleting an entry meant ⌘K → Delete, then confirming. The user asked for
smaller rows, one status line that stays put, and a quicker delete: ⌘⌫ and a × on hover.

## Implementation

**Compact rows beside a preview.** `RowsView.Metrics` holds a row's measures. `regular` is Spotlight's, used
by search and full-width lists. `compact` is 38 pt, a 20 pt icon and a 14 pt title, and is what a list with
`preview` uses, now in 40% of the width. History rows dropped their "Ghostty · 5 min ago" subtitle and are a
title alone. Search's Clipboard rows keep it (`ClipboardHost.subtitle`). A 0.5 pt separator-colour hairline
between the list and the preview matches Files' columns.

**A status line on details.** `DetailNode.status` is a list of short facts that dock2 joins with " · " in an
11 pt secondary line along the foot, under a rule, outside the scroll view, so it stays as the body scrolls.
It is in the protocol and the Rust SDK (`Detail::status`) rather than a clipboard-only patch. Plugin details
have the same kind of fact, and `metadata` stays for label/value content such as a URL or a username.
Folding metadata into a status line was rejected: links and tags don't fit on one line. The clipboard's status
is source, relative time, and size (characters, pixels, or a file count). A colour's sRGB metadata became
`rgb(…)` under the hex in the body. A preview's body is 13 pt; a detail view keeps 14.

**Centring an image.** An image and its text are measured as drawn (`shownImageHeight`, and
`NSAttributedString.boundingRect` for the body, which avoids touching the text view's layout manager and its
TextKit 1 fallback) and centred in the room above the foot when they fit. Centring text alone was tried and
reverted at the user's call: short text floating mid-pane read worse than text at the top.

**⌘⌫ and the ×.** `Chord` now parses `delete`. `Chord.remove` (⌘⌫) is the convention: an item action bound to
it is offered on the item's row as a × while the pointer is over the row. The row is a `Hoverable`, lit by
the panel's pointer monitor, since a tracking area misses moves while dock2 isn't the active app. Only the
hovered row's title shortens to make room, so other titles don't give up width to a mark that isn't shown.
Clicking the × deletes that row's item, selected or not. When the selected item goes, the selection passes
to the one that takes its place rather than jumping to the newest. Plugins still can't bind ⌘⌫:
`PluginChord` allows letters only, and the text-editing keys are dock2's. A menu draws the character ⌫ types
(0x7F) as ⌦, so `Chord.menuKey` shows backspace's instead.

**Delete no longer asks; ⌘Z undoes it.** A confirmation pill on every × click would undo the point. A copy can
be made again, so neither the history's Delete nor search's asks first, and both lose the red destructive
style. Search keeps ⌘⇧D for it, because ⌘⌫ there edits the query, which matters more in search than in the
history's filter. In the history, Esc still clears the filter. In the history, a delete keeps what it took
(`ClipboardController.Deletion`: the entry, its index, its data read before the file is removed) and
registers its restore with `ViewContext.undoManager`, the stage window's. The field's typing registers there
too, so ⌘Z through the main menu's Undo undoes whichever came last, with no key handling of its own.
Restoring registers the delete again, which gives ⇧⌘Z. The undo manager holds its targets unowned, so the
host drops its registrations in `closed()`. The undo therefore lasts while the history is open, so an old
delete can't come back in a later visit. `ClipHistory.restore` won't put back an entry that was copied again
meanwhile, and an entry whose data couldn't be read stays deleted rather than coming back unpasteable. Search's
Clipboard rows have no undo.

## Observations

- Driven live through `dock2 debug`: ⌘⌫ deleted the selected test entry and the selection moved to its
  neighbour; the × showed on a hovered, unselected row, and clicking it twice deleted two entries in a row,
  each sliding under the pointer; the status line, the centred image, the top-aligned text and ⌘K's ⌘⌫ were
  captured. Four throwaway entries were copied for the test and deleted, and the previous top entry was
  copied back. ⌘Z was driven the same way: ⌘⌫, ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z and ⌘Z on a throwaway entry each did what they say. A
  delete, then closing and reopening the history, then ⌘Z, did nothing, and dock2 kept running.
- `dock2 debug key` sent a shifted letter's `charactersIgnoringModifiers` unshifted, so ⇧⌘Z never reached the
  main menu's Redo ("Z"). A keyboard puts ⇧ in both, and the router now does too (`ControlRouter.post`).
- A row's hover state isn't recomputed when its list scrolls under a still pointer. The × follows the pointer
  again at its next move.
- A restored entry comes back at its place but isn't selected. The selection stays where the delete left it.
- The ⌘K menu's animation and discoverability came up as their own problem, out of scope here.
- Text copied from editors that don't mark copies concealed, such as a private key from Zed, is kept like any
  copy. Per-app ignoring covers it today.
