# Dark mode, measured

> Change `1792474569` — 2026-10-19

## Goal

Make dark mode as close to the real Dock as light mode. The dark platter was an estimate, and the dot and
label colours were conventions rather than measurements.

## Implementation

**Measuring.** The appearance was switched with SkyLight's `SLSSetAppearanceThemeLegacy` (no Automation
grant needed) and restored afterwards. In dark mode the wallpaper changes, and a terminal window overlapped
the Dock's area, so neither the desktop nor its windows were a stable backdrop. The calibration window gained
a second mode that draws a frozen screenshot of the desktop; it reproduces the desktop exactly, and the real
Dock and every prototype were photographed over the same pixels — that image, and the stripe pattern.

**The platter.** Dark mode's tone curve is not linear: mid-greys pass nearly unchanged while white comes out
at 175, so a white/black pair cannot express it. The filter's `MaxLuma` clamp can: the fit settled on white
0.7812, black 0.06, saturation 1.2, `MaxLumaSDR` 0.8875 — stripes within 2.7 levels, wallpaper edges within
4.9. Blacks land ~7 levels high; that is the residual the curve's shape leaves. The soft rim had to be
dimmer: the rims composite as white, so the same light over darker glass reads brighter.

**The dot.** In dark mode it is additive: +124 on every channel, over the wallpaper and over a grey stripe
alike. A plus-lighter compositing filter reproduces that, but adds about 19 more than the grey it is given,
so the grey is 105.

**The label, in both appearances.** Light mode's label had been checked only against the smooth wallpaper,
where AppKit's regular recipe matches its fill. Over the stripes the real label is much sharper: the regular
recipe's blur-fill pass smears it, and no blur radius undoes that. Fitted instead from the platter's family
(no blur-fill), the label is within ~10 levels over both backdrops in light mode (regular: 22 over the
stripes) and ~7–8 in dark. The comparison also showed the capsule's bottom half a point high: it is now 26
tall, 9 above the platter, with the arrow at 7.25 and the text's baseline unchanged on screen. Dark text is
white at 95%, measured at 245 on the fill.

## Observations

- The light label's real rim peaks a pixel inside the edge; a key-fill highlight peaks on the edge itself, so
  the fit chose almost no rim. The label reads slightly flatter than the Dock's at the top and bottom edges.
- Dark mode was measured on this MacBook's SDR panel. `MaxLuma` is set equal to `MaxLumaSDR` in dark mode, as
  AppKit's own dark recipes do; how the Dock treats an HDR display is unverified.
- The calibration tooling still lives in a scratch directory, not the tree.
