# A controlled pour: the field's contents ride inside its glass

> Change `1792869276` — 2026-10-24

## Goal

Typing the first letter poured the field's form out into the field with a jumpy, unpredictable quality,
the magnifier leaving the glass as it moved from the middle to the left. Measured offline from `FieldMotion`
(the motion `FormDriver` plays) over 63 moments of each form's rest, and on screen at 50 fps with the lava
lamp:

- The lava lamp's magnifier rode whichever flung droplet went left, up to 25 pt ahead of the body's left end
  in the first 40–190 ms. The droplet streaks thin, so the magnifier hung half over the desktop. The drop's
  magnifier sprang from the middle faster than the body opened, up to 28 pt ahead of it.
- The magnifier passed its place by 9–17 pt and came back, and from some moments of the lava lamp's rest it
  first went up to 30 pt right, because the droplet it followed began right of it.
- In every form the body's ends sprang 10–13 pt past the field's ends and back.
- The lava lamp's text, clamped only against the union of shapes (which a droplet led), could pass the body,
  and it landed in the middle under the magnifier, up to 44 pt of overlap.
- The lava lamp's two droplets and the drop's two fronts rode ahead of the body's ends to the field's ends,
  outside the body until 0.33 s and up to 81 pt out after the first 0.1 s. Merging early reads as the form's
  droplets joining the bar; still hanging off it later, they read as imprecision. Reviewing the recording, the
  aim became: droplets visibly standing out of the bar's ends through the sixth frame on screen, and one clean
  capsule by the seventh, 0.1 s after the letter.

## Implementation

The field's outline is treated as a mold: the glass fills it and stops at its ends, and what the field holds
is carried inside the glass.

- **Ends.** The drop's, gem's and lava lamp's ends pour out critically damped at response 0.42, as `Growth`'s
  in-going edges already are. That keeps the old peak speed (~1.6k pt/s) and settles within 1 pt 40–90 ms
  sooner (0.53 s against 0.57–0.62 s), though the last 2% is a softer landing: 98% at 0.40 s against 0.26–0.28 s.
  0.36 would land quicker, at about 20% more peak speed.
- **Droplets and fronts ride the body's ends.** Each is placed (`Spring.place`) at a distance past its end. That
  distance is a spring which gathers the droplet from wherever the rest left it (response 0.05), onto a schedule
  of the time since the letter (`FormSpace.lead`). For a droplet's middle, the schedule is 8 pt inside the end,
  so the droplet stands half out, until 0.055 s. It then sinks on a smoothstep to `FormSpace.tucked` by
  0.095 s. Tucked is the droplet's half-length plus 14 pt: deeper than the field's glass melts shapes together
  (`GlassRecipe` smoothness 12). Tucked only 2 pt in, a droplet swells the tip by about 2 pt under a quadratic
  smooth union, settled or not. The lava lamp's droplets are tucked by their most-stretched length
  (`Blob.stretchMost`), because a droplet riding a fast end is drawn up to 1.55 times its size; without that, a
  0.7 pt swell lingered to 0.24 s.
- **Two versions were rejected on the way.** Following a point inside the end, as a critically damped
  follower, trailed a fast end by tens of points, so the droplets were swallowed by 0.05 s and barely showed.
  Flinging them toward the field's ends slower than the body opens (response 0.52) let the body overtake
  them about on time. But whether a bubble showed depended on where the rest left each droplet: usually only
  one end had a bubble, and 11 of 63 moments showed none. Riding the end makes the bubbles a schedule. Both
  ends show one on every pour, about 16, 15 and 10 pt out at the fourth, fifth and sixth frames.
- **What went away with that.** The lava lamp's clamps (the 14 pt parting and `x.velocity *= 0.5`), the drop's
  two clamps on its fronts, and both forms' sizing of their droplets for leading. The drop's lumps swell as
  `(1 - progress)²` on a quicker spring, so its middle bulges only as it starts to open.
- **Carry.** Each `FormSim` says where its body's left end carries the magnifier (`carried(space:)`, built on
  `FormSpace.carried`: its inset from the end, or the body's middle while the body is narrower). The drop's
  and lava lamp's magnifiers follow it, and so does `FieldMotion`'s text at its 29 pt gap, all on critically
  damped springs of one response (`FormSpace.carry`, 0.06). A follower of a critically damped target never
  overshoots and lags toward the inside. Because both followers share the target and response, the text and
  magnifier move as one. The gem keeps its own way: its magnifier never travels, but shows at its place once
  the glass passes it.
- **The text lands beside the magnifier**, not in the middle. A first pass left the text on its own slower
  spring under a floor at the magnifier's gap. The overlap was gone, but where the floor let go mid-pour the
  text, already fully visible, shed ~360 pt/s in one 240 Hz tick: a hitch. Following the same target removed
  the floor, and with it the union-of-shapes clamp in `FieldMotion.frame`.
- The drop steps its magnifier after its body, as `FieldMotion` steps the text after the sim. Retargeting from
  the body before its step put the two followers a tick apart, about 6 pt at speed, which the new test caught.

After: every shape stays within the field's ends; the magnifier's ink stays inside the body, never passes its
place, and goes right less than 1 pt in all; the text keeps its gap. `itPoursWithinTheField` checks all of
that for each form from 18 moments of its rest, and replaces `theTextNeverLeadsTheGlass`. Modelling the melt
as a quadratic smooth union 12 pt across, no droplet stands out of the body after 0.090 s in any moment of
either form's rest, the melt leaves at most 0.6 pt at 0.1 s, and the settled field has nothing on its outline.
`itsDropletsMeltInByATenthOfASecond` checks both sides: a droplet out of each end at 0.058 s, and none
from 0.1 s on. The lava lamp's peak
magnifier acceleration fell from 124k to 53k pt/s² and the text's from 266k to 53k. Recorded before and after
on the real glass with the debug build at each step, the lava lamp shows what the probe does.

## Observations

- The spec's "the letter lands in its middle" became "beside the magnifier"; `product/panel.md` says so.
- The lava lamp's body still swells to about 60 pt tall, past the field's 57, as `big.d` settles (damping 0.45).
  This is the squash the forms are meant to have, and it is vertical, so it was left.
- The drop's splash, thrown above the field by each later letter, is a designed flourish and is untouched.
- Emptying the field was not retuned, and it has the mirror image of the pour's droplets. The bar draws in
  faster than its droplets, which are left up to 160 pt outside it (lava lamp; 41 pt for the drop's fronts)
  for about 0.6 s as they drift back, exactly as before this change. Droplets parting is the lava lamp's
  resting character, so whether this reads as sloppy is a judgement still to make.
- The probes and the before/after filmstrips were scratch harnesses compiled with `swiftc` against the Core
  sources, as `OBSERVING.md` describes.
