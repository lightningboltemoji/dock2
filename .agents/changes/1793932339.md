# Scrollers stay overlaid

> Change `1793932339` — 2026-11-05

## Goal

A bar stood down the right of the search results, cutting the selection's rounded end, there even over "No results". It
showed in a VM on Tahoe and on macOS 27 alike, so it had nothing to do with `1793931389`, and it had never been seen on the
Mac dock2 is built on.

## Implementation

It was a scroller track. Five scroll views set `scrollerStyle = .overlay` and took it for settled, but AppKit writes the
style again afterwards: `legacy`, twice in a run, under the default "Show scroll bars: Automatic". A legacy track stands
whether anything scrolls or not, and `hasVerticalScroller` is on for every list. `OverlayScrollView` answers those writes
with overlay, and the five use it in place of setting the style themselves. Setting `autohidesScrollers` was the smaller
change and is rejected: it hides the track only until a list overflows, and the track would then cut the rows.

The bar reproduced on a macOS 27 VM and went away under "When scrolling", which is what named it. With the class in place
it is gone under Automatic from a list that fits and one of seven rows, on 27.2 and on 26.6.2, and Files is unchanged.

## Observations

Who else saw the bar is inferred: a VM's pointer stands in for a mouse, so a Mac with a mouse and no trackpad probably did,
but no such Mac was tried. Actually scrolling a list long enough to overflow was not driven, so the overlay scroller
appearing as it scrolls is unseen, as are the form, detail and grid components, which take the same class.

Measured alongside it, the glass's face over solid white and black on both VMs, the same build and the same virtual GPU:
light glass reads 228 and 38 on both, dark over black 15 on both, and dark over white 179 on macOS 27 and 199 on Tahoe. The
recipe's note has 175 on real hardware, so a VM's glass is near but not exact. Tahoe's 199 is the dark recipe's `White`,
0.7812, with nothing capping it: that is the missing `inputFaceColorMatrixMaxLuma`, which settles the question `1793931389`
left open. Only the face was measured; the edges, where the halo, ring shadow and in-filter highlight act, were not.
