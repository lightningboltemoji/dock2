# The form's glass never turns

> Change `1792959802` — 2026-10-25

## Goal

The lava lamp's bubbles wobbled: they flashed lighter and deflected for a moment, at rest and through pours and
emptying. `1792869276` and `1792896598` retuned its springs without touching it. This change measured what was on
screen first.

- **At rest.** 26 s of the resting lamp, recorded at 120 fps over a flat backdrop window, each frame scored by how far
  it departs from the average of its two neighbours. Flashes stood out at 1.9, 7.9, 13.9, 19.9 and 25.9 s, two frames
  each and two grey levels brighter, with smaller ones 0.7 s after each. In the flash frames the body's shadow and its
  tight dark ring were gone.
- **The cause.** The body and the droplets were capsules turned by `atan2` of their velocity, snapping to no turn
  below 9 pt/s. The glass turns an element by its transform, which Core Animation interpolates by angle, the short way
  round. The shadow and its mask are paths, whose points it interpolates in straight lines. Halfway between a sample
  turned 2.405 rad and an unturned one, the shadow path reached 36% of its size. In the loop that happened at 4.2 s and
  every 6 s after, `big.x`'s period. A turn from 0 to −1.06 rad at 4.9 s shrank it to 86%. Both match the recording to
  the frame.
- **Transitions.** From 18 moments of the rest, there were 168 ticks at 240 Hz in which a droplet 1.3–1.9 times as long
  as it is wide turned 0.35–3.14 rad. A droplet's width also jumped by up to 16 pt in one tick (30 to 46 pt) when `ride`
  set its velocity at the pour.
- **Ruled out, measured.** Core Animation's transform interpolation across ±π takes the short way. A turned
  `CASDFElementLayer` is drawn correctly at eight angles, alone or merged (scratch harness on the field's recipe).
  `CGPath(roundedRect:)` emits the same elements for a circle as for a capsule. The SDF glass draws circular and
  continuous corners alike for capsules and circles. The loop's in-between at 20 Hz is within 0.03 pt of the motion.

## Implementation

The bake was sound. The values it handed Core Animation were not: an angle has no in-between that every layer
following it agrees on, and a value drawn from a velocity jumps wherever the velocity is set rather than reached.

- **Nothing a form plays can turn.** `FormShape` has no rotation, and `FormPose` no `bodyRotation`. `GlassLayer.play`
  no longer plays a transform and asserts that nothing it plays turns. `PanelShadow.play` builds unturned paths.
  `GlassLayer.Shape.rotation` stays for `setShapes`, where the label's arrow is turned and never interpolated.
- **The lava lamp is drawn from where things are.** Each droplet has a trail: a point on a critically damped spring
  of response π·d/900, which lags it by its size at 900 pt/s, as the old stretch did. It is drawn as three discs on a
  line through it toward the trail, the ends 1.35 times the lag apart. The lag is clamped to `stretchMost` of its size,
  and the discs thin by 0.35 of the stretch, so its bounds are the old streak's. On the real glass two discs pinch into
  a peanut from a stretch of 0.3, while three match the turned capsule at every stretch. The body is always an unturned
  capsule, never narrower than it is tall: a disc at rest. It loses its drift's stretch of at most 3%, and with it the
  branch between capsule and streak.
- **The sample rates are Core's** (`FieldMotion.sampleRate`, `loopRate`), so a test can hold the keyframes to them.
- **Tests.** `nothingItDrawsJumps` runs at 240 Hz from eleven moments of the rest, through the rest, a pour with
  growth, a letter, emptying, and widening to Files. At every sample, a copy advanced 1 µs moves no edge more than
  0.05 pt. The old lamp moved 0.65 pt at the pour; the new one moves at most 0.0055 pt, during the growth to Files.
  `itsLoopIsDrawnAsItMoves` checks that, at the loop's 20 Hz, the midpoint of two samples is within 0.1 pt of the motion
  there (0.024 pt measured). The old code passed it too, because its fault was in the angle each frame carried. With no
  angle left, a frame is everything drawn.
- **Rejected.** Mending the angle: unwrapping it, or dropping the snap. The snap was one of several ways it broke,
  alongside the wrap at ±π, sweeps near zero speed, and velocities set by `place`, and a path turned far enough between
  two samples shrinks whatever the cause. Driving the glass from a display link instead of baking would not have
  helped either: the encoding, not the bake, was at fault, and the bake still keeps the motion off a busy main thread.

## Observations

- Filmed after, twice for 26 s at rest: nothing periodic. The 99th percentile of departure fell from 0.81 to 0.29 and
  the largest from 1.53 to 0.65. That largest is one step where the whole cluster moved a pixel further than its
  neighbours, edges and magnifier alike: one late frame, not a change of shape.
- Three pours and three emptyings filmed: a droplet stands out of each end as one drop, the bar draws in to the bead
  with its shadow ring steady, and the droplets bud off.
- **Not addressed: a pour's first frame on screen is already 50–67 ms into it.** In the first frame after the letter
  the glass is 154–247 pt wide, where the motion is 56 pt one frame in. `FormDriver.change` begins its track at the
  media time it was baked, and the commit lands about 55 ms later, so the window server starts mid-track. This
  mechanism is separate from the wobble. The levers are beginning the track when it is committed, or baking before
  search's work on that turn.
- The drop's and the gem's shapes never turned. The gem's faces are polygons, whose corners do interpolate in straight
  lines.
- A track's corner curve is its last sample's: continuous for a whole track that ends grown. The glass draws the
  two curves alike here, but the clip is a plain layer and may not.
- The recordings, the glass harness and the probes were scratch, compiled against the Core sources as `OBSERVING.md`
  describes, and are not kept.
