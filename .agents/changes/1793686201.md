# Views read from the top, with a bar of their keys

> Change `1793686201` — 2026-11-02

## Goal

Bitwarden, run against a mock server, showed the views' rough edges. Typing in a list moved the selection to
whichever row took the old one's place rather than to the best match: "li" left Linear by the field and
Netflix selected above it. A list in a view reads A to Z, which search's bottom-up rows turned upside down,
with A by the field and Z at the far end. Nothing showed a view's keys, so Bitwarden wrote "⌘⇧C copies it"
into its own detail and masked a password with a string of dots. A form that became a list through
`updateView` stayed a form on screen, which is the path Bitwarden's sign-in takes into the vault. An update
naming no open view went to the plugin's top view, which only that bug's path relied on.

## Implementation

**Reading direction.** `RowsView.fromTop` flips its document, so offsets run from the top and `tile` and
`revealSelection` work unchanged. Two AppKit details needed handling. An `NSClipView` reads its document's
flippedness when it is given the document, so the document is handed over again as it flips; without that,
the top rows went untiled and blank. A flipped view's layer is `geometryFlipped`, measured with a probe, so a
moved row's slide offset is negated. An arriving row animates its content layer, inside the unflipped row,
and needs nothing. `ListComponent` puts a section's title before its rows, and the arrows follow the reading.
The grid's canvas flips the same way. Search keeps reading up from the field.

**Selection.** `ListComponent.filter` marks the query as changed, and the next render selects the first
row, the best match. A redraw for the same query keeps the selection, or gives its place to the next row
along as before. With `filtering: owner`, the owner's answer is the render that consumes the mark.

**The bar.** One pill at the field's trailing end, `ActionsBubble`, says "Actions ⌘K", or a form's submit
with ⌘↩. It is a capsule of the label glass the menu is made of, which gives the click a visible edge and
keeps the morph one piece of glass. A first pass also put the selection's primary action beside Actions. That repeated the menu's first
row, and a detail's buttons as well, so the bar now offers only the menu. Components return `ViewActions`
(`.menu` of an `ActionSet` with a perform closure, or `.submit`), and `NodeScreen` forwards them to
`ViewsController.dress`, which runs whenever a screen changes; components now call `onChange` when the
selection moves. ⌘K reaches the bar through `ViewsController.keyEquivalent`, and the components' own ⌘K
`NSMenu`s are gone, so a click and ⌘K open the same menu. Search's and the dock's menus are still `NSMenu`.

The menu borrows the shape of fiber's downloads bubble: the capsule grows into a panel and what is behind
recedes. Unlike fiber's, the pill doesn't stay on as a footer: its label fades as the glass grows and the
rows fill all of it, since a footer naming the menu said nothing the open menu didn't. It is the toast's
label glass over a `PanelShadow`. Its right and bottom edges sit 5 pt out from the pill's, and its corner
radius is 19 pt, so the pill's corners and the panel's are all concentric with it. The springs are the
panel's own `GrowthMotion`, not fiber's: response 0.30 with damping 0.78 opening, and critically damped at
0.22 closing, which is about the same feel. One spring is sampled at 120 Hz into `Keyframes` that the
glass, its shadow and the rows' mask all play, so the three can't drift apart the way three separate
`CASpringAnimation`s could. A change of mind mid-flight starts from the sampled shape at that moment. The
rows are laid out at the open size and uncovered by the mask as it grows. Closing, the label returns from
30% of the way, so the capsule never shows empty. The screen's view dims to 0.58 rather than the content
layer, whose opacity `FormDriver.reveal` animates.

While open, keys go to the menu through `InteractionController.keyActivity`, the hook a confirmation uses to
take ↩ and Esc from whatever has focus. That needs no first-responder juggling across a list's field, a
form's controls and a detail. A key the menu doesn't use closes it and returns false, so typing carries on
into the field. The pill and the rows are `Hoverable`, so the panel's pointer monitor lights them, and the
pointer moves the selection as it does in a menu.

**Masked rows.** `DetailNode.Metadata.masked` draws eight dots, kerned, whatever the value, and the SDK's
`Detail::masked(label)` sends no value. The secret stays in the plugin, as it did when Bitwarden sent the
dots itself. A reveal would need the value sent; it is not built.

**Changing kind, and naming the view.** `NodeScreen.update` builds the component for a node of another kind
and takes the old one out of the panel. The stack sees the screen's view out of its superview and mounts it
again. `PluginsController` sends an update only to the open view whose id matches, and logs a refusal
otherwise. A view's id therefore names it for as long as it is open. Bitwarden's sign-in, codes, unlock and
vault now share the id `bitwarden`, as Now Playing's one view already had, and a submit is read by the form
the plugin has on show. Fixing a screen's kind and pushing a new view for any other kind was the
alternative, but a sign-in would then leave a stack to Esc back through.

## Observations

Seen against the mock:
- The vault from the top, "li" selecting Linear, and ↓ moving to Netflix.
- The form's "Sign In ⌘↩" pill, and the sign-in turning into the vault through the strict update.
- The menu over Clipboard History and the vault: ⌘K and a click both open it, the arrows move in it, Esc and
  a click elsewhere close it, typing closes it and filters, and ⌘⇧L from inside it locks.
- Recorded at 120 fps, opening settles in about 0.2 s and closing in about 0.33 s, each a smooth curve. The
  rectangle the recording shows where the menu was is ScreenCaptureKit's redraw artifact: stills straight
  after a close don't show it.

Not seen: a detail as a view of its own (Now Playing's), a grid, and Reduce Motion's crossfade. A menu taller than
the panel would run off its top, since the rows don't scroll. VoiceOver reads a masked row as its dots, and
the menu's rows as menu items, not heard. `debug state` lists no rows for a view's list, and nothing of the
bar's menu, so both were read from screenshots.
